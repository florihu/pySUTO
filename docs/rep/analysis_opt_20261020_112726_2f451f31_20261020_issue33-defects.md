## 1. Change

Each year's QP is `min Σ((a − a_prior)/σ_a)² + Σ_soft(ε/σ_c)²` under hard rows, soft rows with a free slack and
lower bounds. Solved for the flows `a`, Clarabel's cost is `J − Σ(a_prior/σ_a)²`: on 2015 of
`const_20261019_170151_13460e94` the constant is 1,288,065 against an optimal J of 117,282, so the relative gap
is measured against a number ten times the misfit, and the dual cost at the stop bounds nothing (−3e6 to −2.6e7 on
the probe). `QP_VARIABLES = "defects"` (#33, `1f2965c`) solves the same QP for `d = a − a_prior` (same P and A,
`q = 0`, `b = c − G·a_prior`), so Clarabel's cost is J itself; F_opt comes back as `a_prior + d`, and the
sidecar's `objective` stays in the flows convention under both. The measured change sets that knob and
`TOL_GAP_REL = TOL_GAP_ABS = 1e-8` together — the maintainer's decision (2026-10-20): at the ADR 0039 tolerances
the defects form stops short of the lower bounds and Stage 5 would see a third fewer bound-active edges (2015 of
the probe: 6,537 against 9,794).

The read-only probe behind it (`scripts/explo/issue33_conditioning_probe.py`, table
`docs/rep/figs/issue33/probe_const_20261019_170151_13460e94.csv`) solved six years three ways and bounded each
answer from below with the Lagrange dual at its own multipliers, a bound valid whatever the residuals (re-derived
independently in review for 2015: J 117,281.867, LB 117,281.7825). The defects form reached Solved at 1e-8 in
every probed year within 0.2 of that bound; the base sits 945–4,363 above it; a whitened form (P = 2I) was
rejected (2015 AlmostSolved, hard-row residuals up to 3e-2 kt).

## 6. Reading

- **The base is the inexact answer, now with a certificate.** Its posteriors sit 496–4,362 above the defects
  form's in every year, and the probe bounds the defects form within 0.2 of the optimum. Rounds measured on the
  flows form compared answers whose termination error was of that size (−66,702 over the run, −2.13 % of Σ_t J
  vs `opt_20261019_171746_3272682b`), so Δz² below a few percent between earlier runs was not resolvable.
- **The weakly held Mass directions were set by the stop, not the model.** World crude-ore Mass retention
  0.864 → 0.998 and Chile's 2015 ore 391,803 → 732,602 kt (prior 732,723): the manuscript's A5 ledger row
  ("termination accuracy moves ore-mass retention 0.779 → 0.861") described how far the flows form stopped from
  its optimum, not a modelling property.
- **Items 3 and 5 fail at Clarabel's numerical floor, not on quality.** Item 3: 1.05–1.62e-6 kt on equality
  rows in 3 years, not reducible by `tol_feas` 1e-9; item 5: a slack threshold of 1e-6 kt on a fringe both
  runs share. Neither moves J, the anchors or retention. The durable fix for item 5 is a binding test that does
  not depend on a slack threshold (Stage 5 reading the QP's multipliers), a Stage-5 change of its own.
