## 4 · Honest reading of the residual

Round F does **not** return the totals to baseline: +1.4 % z² and Conc-CC / Smelt-CC retention
2.5 pp below Round C. That residual is not a pathology — the collapse signature is gone and no
node sits at the −56 z wall any more. It is the cost of carrying 28.1 Mt of additional ICSG
data that the reconciliation must now move priors to accommodate: retention measures how much
of the prior survives, and there is materially more prior to survive.

Two things are worth saying plainly:

- **The remaining 36 contradicted node-years are pre-existing and untreated.** They were in
  the shipped model before any of this work; ADR 0019's detector now localises them. Whether
  their treatment is worth a round is an open question, and it is the largest single lead left.
- **Whether +1.4 % z² / −2.5 pp retention is worth the data is a judgement call**, and it is the
  user's, not this REP's. The alternative is reverting ADR 0018 and discarding 28.1 Mt of sound
  recovered data to avoid the 1,530 kt of unsound data that ADR 0020 already withholds.
