## 0 — Executive summary

The collapse is much worse than reported: `a₀` ranges over `[0, 1.7e7]`, `F_opt` ranges over `[0, 0.15]` — an 8-order-of-magnitude flattening, with median `|F_opt| ≈ 1e-12` across every solved year. The unit-mixing fix (mass-balance now split by valuation) is intact and is *not* the issue. The collapse is driven by a structural property of the QP as currently parameterised: **`a = 0` is feasible for every homogeneous constraint, and the prior weighting is too weak — and too uneven — to oppose it.** The driver is a four-way conspiracy: (1) 53.6% of edges are imputed by `FlatPriorImputer` with `f = 0` (by design); (2) the Stage-3 relative σ-floor `0.05·|a₀|` inflates σ on high-magnitude edges by 3000× while leaving zero-flow edges alone; (3) `σ_c_abs_floor = 1e-3` weights free-row slack 10⁶× harder than the inflated prior; (4) `RatioConstraintBuilder` never deactivates rows for the wrong year, so all 30 years of `a_i − r·a_j = 0` constraints fight every solve — and `a = 0` satisfies them all. The hard mass-balance at Chile Mining 2024 (which has `Σ_in − Σ_out = −1.76e7` at the prior) is then resolved by collapsing the high-σ_a outflow side to zero rather than lifting the zero-flow inflow side. The warm-start `a₀(t) = F_opt(t−1)` propagates the collapse forward.

> **Verdict.** The collapse is *not* a unit-mixing leak. It is the joint failure of (a) prior coverage at the Mining inflow boundary, (b) the Stage-3 σ_a relative floor making the largest flows the cheapest to move, (c) σ_c floor `1e-3` overwhelming the inflated prior weight by ~10⁶, and (d) ratio_eq rows being active across all timesteps simultaneously — together producing a degenerate optimum at `a ≈ 0`.

## 4 — Pipeline trace of one specific case

**Picked case.** The single worst mass-balance residual: row `mass_balance_node55_t2024_Mass` (Chile Mining, Process, 2024, Mass), `|G a₀ − c| = 1.76e7`. This is one row out of 30 essentially identical ones for Chile mining 1995–2024.

### 4.1 Node 55 mass-balance equation at the prior

Constraint row decomposed:

| edge_id | sign | (from → to) | year/val | a₀ | σ_a (Stage-3 regularised) |
|---:|---:|---|---|---:|---:|
| 538  | +1 | CO/SB/Chile → Mining/Process/Chile | 2024 Mass | **0** | 218,656 |
| 3838 | −1 | Mining/Process/Chile → WR/SB/Chile | 2024 Mass | **1.71e7** | 856,565 |
| 3898 | −1 | Mining/Process/Chile → Ore/Flow/Chile | 2024 Mass | 4.76e5 | 23,853 |

Inflow `= 0`. Outflow `= 1.76e7`. Mass-balance hard row must close this.

### 4.2 Why edge 538 = 0

From [`imputation_log.parquet`](../../data/proc/imp/imp_20260521_074525_b1921faf/imputation_log.parquet):

```
edge_id=538: step=5  method=flat_prior  f_after=0.0  sigma_after=218656
  description: "no data, no imputer matched: flat prior (cv·scale)"
```

`coverage = False` for 2023 and 2024 of this edge (every other year is covered from `crude_ore`). No parametric inverse fires because there is no upstream Cu-in-ore concentration that resolves to a 2024 Chile mining inflow. `TimeImputer` cannot extrapolate (config: `interpolate_only: true`). `FlatPriorImputer` is the terminal fallback and by its definition sets `f = 0`.

### 4.3 Why edge 3838 = 1.71e7

```
edge_id=3838: step=2  method=parametric  f_after=17,130,904  sigma_after=292.8
  source_edges=[3839]  coefficients=[4000.0]
  param_id: conc_wr_use
  description: "Cu concentration in WR (SB) — use edges (to-node=WR/SB)"
```

`ore_grade_*` parametric inverse fired: `a_Mass = 4000 · a_CC` (i.e. waste-rock mass = mass-per-unit-Cu × Cu-in-WR). Imputer-side `σ = 292.8`, **CV ≈ 1.7e-5**. Stage 3's `_lp_regularize` then lifts σ via the relative floor:

```python
# constraints/engine.py:305-315 (σ_a regularisation)
σ_a' = ( σ_a^p + (0.05·|a₀|)^p + (1e-6)^p )^(1/p)
     ≈ (292^1.3 + 856,520^1.3 + ε)^(1/1.3)
     ≈ 856,565
```

So Stage 3 multiplies σ_a by **~2900×**. By design — the README explicitly calls this a *modelling claim*, not a numerical safety net. But the consequence is that the most informative imputer outputs become the cheapest variables to move.

### 4.4 Why the solver picks "collapse outflow" over "lift inflow"

Per-direction prior cost to close the `−1.76e7` imbalance (`Δ²/σ_a²`):

- **Lift inflow 538** by 1.76e7: cost `= (1.76e7 / 218,656)² ≈ 6,480`.
- **Drop outflow 3838** by 1.76e7: cost `= (1.76e7 / 856,565)² ≈ 422`.
- **Drop outflow 3898** by 4.76e5: cost `= (4.76e5 / 23,853)² ≈ 397`.

Collapsing both outflows is **~8× cheaper** than lifting the inflow. The QP picks the cheap direction, and `a = 0` simultaneously satisfies every homogeneous ratio_eq, every homogeneous mass_balance, and every homogeneous ratio_bound row in the entire 30-year stack.

### 4.5 Why this cascades to every other edge in the system

`RatioConstraintBuilder` defines only `build_static` ([`ratio.py:134`](../../src/pysuto/constraints/ratio.py#L134)); it **never overrides `build_dynamic`**, so the engine's deactivation channel (σ_c → `SIGMA_C_INACTIVE = 1e10`) is only used by `SumConstraintBuilder` ([`sum.py:466-497`](../../src/pysuto/constraints/sum.py#L466-L497)). Direct count:

| t | ratio_eq rows active (σ_c < 1e9) | sum_eq rows active |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1995 | **435,690 / 435,690** | 1,316 / 39,480 |
| 2010 | **435,690 / 435,690** | 1,316 / 39,480 |
| 2024 | **435,690 / 435,690** | 1,316 / 39,480 |

So at every timestep, every year's `a_i − r·a_j = 0` is simultaneously enforced with `σ_c` floored at `~ 1e-3` (since `c = 0` → the Lp regulariser leaves σ_c at the abs floor). The slack penalty `(1/σ_c²) = 1e6` is then ~10⁴× the inflated-prior weight even on the *small* edges, and ~10¹⁸× on the Mining edges. The optimum at `a = 0` makes the slack vanish exactly. Once `F_opt(t=1995) ≈ 0`, the warm-start contract `a₀(t) = F_opt(t−1)` propagates the collapse forward forever.

## 5 — Hypotheses

### H1 — Stage-2 coverage gap at the Mining inflow boundary is the trigger

**Mechanism.** `FlatPriorImputer` ([`impute/flat_prior.py`](../../src/pysuto/impute/flat_prior.py)) sets `f = 0` for the 53.6 % of edges with no feed coverage. For Chile Mining 2023/2024 inflow (`crude_ore` feed runs out), this leaves a hard mass-balance with `Σ_in = 0` while outflow `Σ_out = 1.7e7`. Hard equality forces the gap to close somewhere.

**Predicted signature.** Worst mass-balance residuals at the prior should concentrate on `(sector, year)` cells where the feed coverage drops to zero, and the collapse magnitude should track `Σ_out − Σ_in` at the prior.

**Falsifier.** If we re-fill `FlatPrior`-zero inflows with something non-zero (e.g., extrapolate `crude_ore` to 2024 via NOCB) and the collapse persists at the same scale, H1 is rejected.

### H2 — Σ_a relative floor inverts the prior weighting

**Mechanism.** [`engine.py:305-315`](../../src/pysuto/constraints/engine.py#L305-L315) applies `σ_a' = Lp(σ_a, 0.05·|a₀|, 1e-6)`. For imputer outputs with CV < 0.05 (which is most parametric fills), this *inflates* σ by orders of magnitude. The largest-magnitude edges have the largest inflation: edge 3838 σ goes `292 → 856,565`. The prior weight `1/σ_a²` is then driven by the inflation, not by data.

**Predicted signature.** Removing the 0.05 floor (or replacing it with `0.05 · sigma_agg` instead of `0.05·|a₀|`) should shift the optimum away from `a ≈ 0` toward `a ≈ a₀` for high-magnitude edges, at the cost of larger absolute prior σ_a.

**Falsifier.** If we set `sigma_a_cv_min = 1e-3` (essentially off) and `F_opt` still collapses, H2 is rejected.

### H3 — ratio_eq rows never deactivated → cross-year constraint pollution

**Mechanism.** `RatioConstraintBuilder.build_dynamic` is not overridden ([`ratio.py:134`](../../src/pysuto/constraints/ratio.py#L134) only). So at every t, all 435,690 ratio_eq rows across 30 years are σ_c-floored at `~1e-3` and binding. The `a = 0` solution satisfies them all for free.

**Predicted signature.** Adding a `time != t → sigma_c = SIGMA_C_INACTIVE` override (mirroring `sum.py:466-497`) should make off-year ratios stop dragging the solve.

**Falsifier.** If we deactivate ratio_eq cross-year and the collapse persists, H3 is rejected. (It probably is *necessary but not sufficient* — deactivation alone won't fix the in-year mass-balance gap from H1.)

### H4 — σ_c absolute floor `1e-3` is too tight relative to the σ_a floor

**Mechanism.** Free-row slack penalty `1/σ_c² = 1e6` is paired with prior penalty `1/σ_a² ≈ 4e-12` on Mining edges, a 10¹⁸ imbalance. Raising `sigma_c_abs_floor` to `1e0` or making it relative to `|c|·CV_min` would close the gap somewhat.

**Predicted signature.** Bumping `sigma_c_abs_floor` from 1e-3 to 1e0 should reduce the slack-weight dominance on `c = 0` rows.

**Falsifier.** If the absolute floor never fires (i.e. if `0.05·|c|` already dominates) the change is a no-op. Only meaningful for rows where `c = 0`, which is most ratio_eq rows.

### 7.1 Verdict

The collapse is a **structural degeneracy of the QP**, not a bug in any single line. Four conditions coincide:

1. **Feed coverage runs out at the boundary years** (Chile mining inflow at 2023/2024 — `crude_ore` feed has no data) → `FlatPriorImputer` legitimately sets `a₀ = 0`, leaving the hard mass-balance row at the affected node infeasible by the full outflow magnitude (1.7e7).
2. **The Stage-3 relative σ-floor `0.05 · |a₀|`** inflates σ_a on high-magnitude edges by 10³×, making them the cheapest variables to move when closing the hard-row residual.
3. **`RatioConstraintBuilder` never deactivates cross-year rows.** All 30 years' `a_i − r·a_j = 0` are enforced at every solve with σ_c at the absolute floor.
4. **`a = 0` is feasible for every homogeneous row** (mass_balance, ratio_eq, ratio_bound, sum_eq with `time ≠ t`, edge bounds with `l = 0`).

Conditions (2) + (3) + (4) make `a = 0` the cheapest point in the QP whenever (1) creates a feasibility gap that the prior cannot afford to close on its own side. The warm-start contract then propagates the collapse forward, so every year sees a worse prior than the last.

The unit-mixing fix is correct and is not the source of the problem. The user's intuition ("the unit-mixing fix should have prevented this") is mis-attributed.

## 8 — Proposed fixes

Ranked by expected leverage. (1) and (2) are necessary; (3) and (4) are tightening.

### 8.1 (Highest leverage) — Fix the Stage-2 coverage gap, not the Stage-3 floor

**What.** Two parallel sub-fixes:
- (a) Extend `crude_ore` (and any other feed) to cover 2023–2024. If genuine data is unavailable, use `TimeImputer` with `interpolate_only: false` so NOCB extrapolation fills 2024 from 2022 with a `gap_inflation`-bumped σ. Config: [`config/config.yaml:43`](../../config/config.yaml#L43).
- (b) Audit `FlatPriorImputer.scale_cascade` — the 3-level fallback (narrow → broad → global median) should never produce `f = 0` on edges that have *any* sibling observation in the broader scope. If level 3 lands on `|f_agg_global_median| > 0` the fill should reflect that magnitude, not 0. Per the current spec the *flow value* is always 0 by definition; if the goal of the flat prior is to express "no data → don't constrain", the prior should be wide (large σ) **and centred on a regional-median scale**, not zero. Files: [`impute/flat_prior.py`](../../src/pysuto/impute/flat_prior.py).

**Expected effect.** Removes the source of the 1.7e7 mass-balance residual. The solver no longer has a hard-row gap large enough to make collapse the cheap direction.

**Risk.** Choosing a non-zero centre for the flat prior is a modelling decision that affects every uncovered edge — needs review against the design intent in the FlatPrior REP (`docs/rep/analysis_optim_const_copper_…_20260513_112419.md`). The cheapest version is (a) extrapolating the feed; (b) is a deeper change.

### 8.2 (Necessary) — Add `build_dynamic` to `RatioConstraintBuilder`

**What.** Mirror `SumConstraintBuilder.build_dynamic` ([`sum.py:466-497`](../../src/pysuto/constraints/sum.py#L466-L497)) in `RatioConstraintBuilder` to set `σ_c = SIGMA_C_INACTIVE = 1e10` for rows whose `time` ≠ `t`. Each ratio_eq row already carries `time = edge_time[edge_i]` in `row_meta` ([`ratio.py:155`](../../src/pysuto/constraints/ratio.py#L155)) so the wiring is straightforward.

**Expected effect.** ~30× drop in active free rows per solve (`435,690 → ~14,500`). Removes the "all 30 years' ratios are binding at once" pressure. Cross-year contamination through the soft block goes away; the solver now sees one year's ratio_eq at a time.

**Risk.** Tiny. The same deactivation pattern is already used by sum_eq and is proven to be a no-op for valid rows. Test: re-run constraint-engine `_check` ([`engine.py:172-241`](../../src/pysuto/constraints/engine.py#L172-L241)); type counts and unit-coherence checks should be unchanged.

### 8.3 (Cleanup) — Tighten or invert the σ_a CV floor for high-CV imputer outputs

**What.** Today the Lp combiner ([`engine.py:305-315`](../../src/pysuto/constraints/engine.py#L305-L315)) computes `σ_a' = Lp(σ_a, 0.05·|a₀|, 1e-6)`. This always *inflates*. Two options:

- (a) Replace the relative floor with a relative *ceiling* on the imputer-side CV: `σ_a' = min(σ_a, 0.05·|a₀|)`. So the floor still protects against pathologically tight imputer outputs but does not inflate already-loose ones. The "modelling claim" reframes to "we trust σ_a no tighter than 5 % CV".
- (b) Keep the floor but lower it to `sigma_a_cv_min = 0.005` (0.5 %) so that genuine imputer confidence is retained on high-magnitude flows.

**Expected effect.** Prior weight on Mining edges goes from `1/(0.05·1.7e7)² = 1.4e-12` to `1/292² = 1.2e-5` — seven orders of magnitude restoration. Combined with 8.2 this should bring `F_opt` back into the same order of magnitude as `a₀`.

**Risk.** A tighter prior on edges with imputer-tight σ may produce numerical conditioning issues in Clarabel (`Σ_a^{-1}` condition number bumps by ~10⁶). The existing solver-side conditioning (`sigma_a_floor_abs = 1e-8`, `sigma_a_floor_rel = 1e-5`, ceiling factor 1e5) caps this at ~12 orders of magnitude — likely still within Clarabel's tolerance.

### 8.4 (Sanity) — Sanity-check the σ_c floor on free rows with c ≈ 0

**What.** `sigma_c_abs_floor = 1e-3` produces `(1/σ_c²) = 1e6` for any `c ≈ 0` row. Either raise the floor (`1e0`) or replace the Lp regularisation with `σ_c' = max(σ_c, sigma_c_cv_min · |c| + sigma_c_abs_floor)`, i.e. require an explicit `c`-scale to apply CV — currently when `c = 0` the CV term vanishes and only the tiny absolute floor protects against `σ_c = 0` (which would be a division-by-zero).

**Expected effect.** Marginal once 8.2 is in place (active ratio_eq row count is already much smaller), but it removes the 10⁶ weight on homogeneous-row slack and prevents future surprises when new builders create `c = 0` soft rows.

**Risk.** Loosening σ_c on the active per-year rows could make soft constraints "soft enough to ignore". The right value is workload-specific; start with `sigma_c_abs_floor = 1e-1` and inspect `eps_heatmap` panels in `diag/`.
