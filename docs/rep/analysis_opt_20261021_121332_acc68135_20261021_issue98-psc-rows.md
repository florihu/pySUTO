## 3. Why #36 is rejected

Freed from the hidden pin, the eff-less PSC legs absorb what the cathode balances cannot place: the near-pin PSC rows
(σ_c < 0.01 kt, the rows that hold PSC at zero where a region refines nothing) go from Σz² 0.04 to 1,437 — Slovakia
2016 z +37.8, the United Kingdom 1996 +12.8, Slovakia 2002 +7.0 — and the world PSC posterior drifts in the years
those legs move (1996 +16.5 kt CC). The common set falls 2.19 % for that reason, not for a better fit: this is
ADR 0041's "sink, not a fit" again. `EFFLESS_STOCK_SIGMA_KT = 0.0` keeps the pin, now stated in ADR 0041.
