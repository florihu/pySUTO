**Diagnosis.** The recovered rows are overwhelmingly *refinery feed*. Restoring
`Matte→CFR` creates matte demand, which propagates back through the recipe chain
(matte ← smelting ← concentrate ← mining/ore). By [ADR 0006](../adr/0006-scale-total-supersedes-external-control-total.md)
each restored total immediately becomes a **scale-total anchor at `ANCHOR_CV` = 1 %**, so where
the upstream data cannot supply what the restored refinery feed demands, the mismatch is
divided by a very tight σ and shows up as large z. The ICSG rows themselves are internally
coherent (e.g. Russia 2023: smelting 852 → refining 845 kt; Bulgaria 277 → 209; Iran 290 →
241), so this is not a mapping error — it is a genuine conflict between ICSG's refinery-feed
series and the upstream supply the model can find for those regions.

Two observations sharpen it, and both are honest caveats rather than settled conclusions:

1. **The response is not proportional to the restored tonnage.** Russia had only 3 years /
   462 kt recovered yet accounts for ≈ +54,000 z² across Smelting/Cathode/Concentrate/MF/Ore/
   Mining; Bulgaria recovered ~76 kt and gained +10,955 at Smelting. Consistent with the
   cv-1 % anchor mechanism above (small `c`, tight σ, large z), but it means the *pricing* of
   these anchors — not the data — may be doing most of the damage. That is precisely the
   knot [ADR 0016](../adr/0016-provenance-aware-sigma-on-scale-totals.md) left open when it
   kept the provenance-aware σ mechanism but rejected loosening as a default.

   > **Amended 2026-09-08 — this hypothesis is disproven; the mechanism is sharper.**
   > σ_c/|c| is a uniform 0.017 on every sum row, so there is no mispricing to find. The real
   > mechanism is a **contradicted flow node**: the Matte node's mass balance is *hard*, and
   > two tight scale totals sit on either side of it — inflow anchored on ICSG concentrate use,
   > outflow on the restored ICSG refining feed. Since σ_c scales with |c|, the smaller
   > (outflow) side is the cheaper anchor to satisfy, so the solver collapses the node to it:
   > Russia 1995 inflow 545.0 vs outflow 50.8 kt CC costs z ≈ 574 to honour the inflow but only
   > z ≈ 53 to collapse. Option (c) below — measuring ADR 0016's provenance-aware σ — is
   > therefore **not** the fix on its own: loosening the anchor alone only makes the collapse
   > cheaper, and the restored edge's own σ_a blocks equally hard (z ≈ 142 at Russia). Round E
   > acts on both σ's for the 48 contradicted Matte node-years; see
   > [ADR 0019](../adr/0019-contradicted-flow-nodes-lose-anchor-authority.md).
2. **World chain retention fell ~3 pp** at the concentrate and smelting stages (0.758 → 0.729,
   0.710 → 0.683). Cathode holds at 0.991. For the manuscript this is the number that matters
   most, and it moved the wrong way.

## 4 · What this means, and the options

The round achieved its stated goal — 29,648 kt of real ICSG data that the pipeline had been
discarding is now in the model, the fabricated-chain class is down a third, and Kazakhstan's
signature case is fully resolved. It did **not** reduce total tension; it converted hidden
tension (a silently missing production basis) into visible tension (an upstream supply
conflict priced at 1 % σ). Whether that is a good trade is a judgement call, so it is the
user's:

- **(a) Keep as is.** The model now contains data it was throwing away, and the residual
  conflict is a specific, answerable question rather than a silent gap. Cost: +4.3 % z²,
  −3 pp Conc/Smelt retention.
- **(b) Keep, and chase the upstream conflict next** — do Iran/Russia/Bulgaria/Zambia have the
  mining and concentrate-trade data to feed the smelter series ICSG reports? This is the
  natural Round E and the reason the recovery is worth keeping: it *asks* the question.
- **(c) Revisit the anchor pricing first** (ADR 0016's open follow-up): if the +4.3 % is mostly
  the cv-1 % scale anchor on small restored totals rather than a real data conflict, a
  provenance- or size-aware σ on scale totals would absorb it. Observation 1 above is
  suggestive but not proof — it needs the ADR-0016 mechanism switched on and measured.
- **(d) Revert to ADR 0008's bundle-total soft row** — preserve the national total as one soft
  constraint without asserting the domestic split. Keeps the total, avoids forcing the recipe
  chain, but costs the new feed→constraint path ADR 0008 declined to build.

**Recommendation: (b), with (c) measured first** — (c) is cheap (the mechanism already exists
and is inert) and would tell us how much of the +4.3 % is pricing rather than substance. The
transit/netting half of Round C's original Round-D proposal remains untouched and unaffected
(hub class identical), and is still available as a later round.

*Taiwan's 15.9 Mt remains deliberately undistributed; recovering it requires the BACI-490
("Other Asia, nes") mapping fix, which is a separate change (ADR 0018 §Considered options).*
