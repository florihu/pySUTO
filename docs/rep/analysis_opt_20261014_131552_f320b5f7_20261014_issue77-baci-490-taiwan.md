## 1. Change

#77 maps BACI code 490 ("Other Asia, nes", Taiwan's trade in UN Comtrade and BACI) to Taiwan instead of ROW.
- **Before:** Taiwan was trade-blind, and its ICSG fans stayed undistributed (ADR 0018). ROW's imports carried
  Taiwan's cathode: 375–658 kt a year in 1995–2011 in baci v18. That gave ROW's domestic semis share a zero
  weight (#76).
- **The guard.** The netting maps codes through its source version's region map, and #77's guard refuses a
  source whose map differs from `current/`'s. So this chain starts at the BACI re-parse.
- **What the re-parse changes.** The re-parse is code-level. It reproduces v13's `values.csv` and
  `quantity_recon.csv` exactly. Only the ADR 0033 σ sidecar changes: 56 root-leg keys, every one touching ROW
  or Taiwan, with the same total mass.

## 4. Taiwan

Section T. All flows are CC, summed over 1995–2024.

- **ICSG reports only use for Taiwan:** 30 `Semi` rows and 11 `MESC` rows. It reports no Taiwanese production.
- **Semis use.** The base books 3 kt CC against ICSG's 15,460, with a prior of 0. The new run gives prior
  15,460 and posterior 14,820: 0.959 of ICSG's figure, 0.67–1.25 by year.
- **Cathode imports.** Prior 15,566, posterior 14,995. The largest origins are Chile 5,439, Japan 3,252,
  Australia 1,689, China 1,290 and Peru 699.
- **Concentrate.** Imports go from prior 557 to posterior 80, so the solver deletes 477 kt CC: Taiwan has no
  process to use it (§8). Exports go from prior 40 to posterior 50, mostly to India 17 and China 12.
- **Anode and matte that Taiwan never produced.** The new prior carries Taiwan's BACI exports of anode (104,
  posterior 46, mostly to China 37) and matte (87, posterior 86: China 22, ROW 21, Australia 20). To supply
  them, the solver opens legs whose prior is 0:
  - matte imports, 34 kt CC;
  - scrap into Taiwan's `CFR` and `Smelting`, 11 and 10 kt CC.

  This is ADR 0014's "noisy-zero scrap faucet", small but present; the base had none of it.

## 5. ROW: the gap the base hid

Section N reads ROW's cathode rows in the re-export ledger. ROW is excluded from the netting (`HUB_EXCLUDE`),
so these are its unnetted totals. The quantity is ICSG's ROW cathode use U (semis, USC, MESC) minus ROW's
cathode balance, ICSG production P plus BACI imports I less BACI exports X. U − (P + I − X), kt CC:

| ledger | 1995–2011 | 2012–2024 |
|---|---|---|
| baci v18 (base; 490 in ROW) | −6,886 (−610 … −293 a year) | +353 (−243 … +344) |
| baci v20 (new; 490 → Taiwan) | **+2,355** (+16 … +261) | **+5,829** (+161 … +752) |

- **Base.** ROW's apparent consumption exceeded ICSG's use in every year 1995–2011. That is the surplus #75
  found refilled onto ROW's domestic semis leg (#76).
- **New.** ICSG reports ROW using more than its production plus imports, less exports, in every year.

Section R gives ROW's domestic semis leg as fed and posterior, beside ROW's production prior less its export
prior, kt CC:

| window | run | fed | posterior | production − exports | semis posterior / ICSG fan | `CP→Cathode` posterior/prior |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1995–2011 | base | 0 | 4,320 | 4,408 | 1.21–1.64 | 0.93–1.06 |
| 1995–2011 | new | 6,194 | 4,324 | 4,210 | 0.73–0.96 | 0.95–1.08 |
| 2012–2024 | base | 774 | 522 | 39 | 0.83–1.11 | 0.65–1.07 |
| 2012–2024 | new | 5,436 | 1,024 | 244 | 0.61–0.89 | 0.98–1.06 |

- **1995–2011: the posterior hardly moves; the prior does.** In the base the imputer wrote ROW's own supply
  onto the leg as its prior, so the posterior agreed with it. Now the prior is ICSG's claim: 6,194 kt CC fed,
  against 4,324 solved. The solver lands where it did before, and the gap shows as z².
- **2012–2024: ROW's refinery output is no longer cut.** ROW's `CP→Cathode` posterior/prior rises from
  0.65–1.07 to 0.98–1.06, and the domestic leg's posterior doubles (522 → 1,024). ICSG's semis claim is still
  far larger: 5,436 fed.
- **Year by year,** the domestic leg's posterior lies near ROW's production less exports in most years (2004:
  314 against 303). It is above it in 2015–2018 (2017: 95 against 42) and 0 in 2019–2023, where ROW's exports
  exceed its production prior.
- **ROW's Δz²** vs `opt_20261013_122432_24e811b7` is +13,765: +5,743 in 1995–2011 and +8,023 in 2012–2024.
  - By sector: Cathode **+18,029**, all of it in the 100–1,000 kt anchor band; CER −1,110, ER −1,110,
    CP −882, CFR −858, Anode −791.

## 10. Consequences

1. **#82, the fix ticket.** Once Taiwan's imports leave ROW, ICSG's ROW cathode use exceeds ROW's production
   plus BACI imports, less exports, by 16–752 kt CC a year. A country-by-country comparison of ICSG's ROW
   members against BACI's imports into the same countries decides which input is wrong. The 18 ROW-bound stock
   legs of §7 belong to that ticket. Until it is resolved, the old chain's apparent fit rested on Taiwan's
   imports cancelling this gap.
2. **Merged over the failed items by the maintainer's decision (2026-10-15).** `opt_20261014_131552_f320b5f7`
   is the reference chain. #75 is the precedent for shipping over a failed item.
   - **For:** Taiwan's 15,460 kt CC of semis use enters the account, and ROW's refinery output after 2011 is
     no longer cut.
   - **Against:** z² rises 1.41 % vs `opt_20261013_122432_24e811b7`, because ROW's gap is now visible
     rather than cancelled.
3. **#78 (#76's observed zero)** runs against this run. Its ROW items (2, and the ROW part of 4) find ROW's
   domestic leg fed and positive, so the measurement turns on the other regions in #76's class.
4. **#71.** This run is the milestone's first production run on the parallel solve; its comparison is
   posted on #71.
