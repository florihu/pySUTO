| P4 (J) | tight 1–3 % above the reference | +2.91 % | right |

P3 missed on the stage whose legs carry the widest σ: on `const_20261124_141044_e73c5952` the CO→Mining Mass legs have a median σ_a of 2,477 kt, and the other eight stages at most 16 kt; their mass-weighted σ/prior is also the largest (0.127 against at most 0.078). This is ADR 0039's mechanism: in the flows form the duality gap is relative to the prior-only constant, so the iterate stops where the widest-σ flows are cheapest to leave. It is the same deletion the A4 expectation was written on (crude-ore Mass retention 0.864, discussion.md L92), and the defects form at 1e-8 removes it.
