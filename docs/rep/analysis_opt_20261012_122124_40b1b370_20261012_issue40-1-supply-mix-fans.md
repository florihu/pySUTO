## 1. Change

Decided 2026-10-12 after #40's REP §5, amended before any measurement (posted on #40):

1. **Stock fans draw from the supply mix** (`568d62f`). A stock change is drawn from the region's
   cathode supply in proportion: own production on the domestic leg, each partner's cathode exports
   to the region on its import leg, read from the route's Cathode→FU leg. A region that refines
   nothing gets no domestic share. A region with neither production nor imports leaves the change
   to the net-stock seed (ADR 0040). Flows keep ADR 0024 exactly as on main.
2. **BACI's cathode fan is 100/0/0** onto FU/USC/MESC (`1da5ced`, #36 item 1). A zero weight gives a
   leg σ 0, which Stage 2 reads as not covered.

**Amendment.** `1da5ced` first read a stock fan's imports from its own BACI legs, which change 2 had
just emptied. trade_shares v25, built that way, had 187 stock groups and none with an import weight,
so no feed was built from it. `568d62f` reads the FU leg instead. v26 has 268 stock groups, all with
import weights; China's 2003 MESC domestic share is 0.595, against 0.725 in v24. v26 changes no
weight outside the Cathode→FU / MESC / USC legs.

**Undistributed by the new rule:** four negative changes, 115.0 kt in total. These are the UAE in 2003
(−59.9) and Singapore in 2003, 2017 and 2018 (−5.2, −29.9, −20.1). Neither refines cathode, and
neither has cathode imports in those years (Singapore's are exhausted by the ADR 0027 netting). The
Taiwan rows stay undistributed for every sign (ADR 0018).

## 3. Item 3: what fails

The stock legs' posteriors follow their priors in all three runs. What changed is the prior:

| MESC + USC, CC, 1995–2024 | prior | posterior |
|---|---|---|
| Z | +6,260 kt | +5,835 kt |
| #40.1 | −398 kt | −609 kt |

Z's prior carried 2 % of all cathode trade as positive stock building, about +209 kt a year. ICSG's
own summary tables put world refined stocks of every type, producers included, at 943 kt in 1995
and 1,399 kt in 2024, a rise of 456 kt over 30 years. So Z's stock legs built about 13 times that
rise. The new prior carries only ICSG's own net additions, and the posterior ends 0.2 Mt below it.

Per year, net (Σ a* − Σ a_prior) and gross (Σ |a* − a_prior|), kt CC:

| | 1995 | 1996 | 2001 | 2003 | 2006 | 2019 | 2022 | max \|net\| | gross range |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Z | −8.2 | −0.1 | −75.3 | −15.0 | 8.2 | −50.3 | −21.5 | 75.3 | 52–172 |
| #40 | −8.2 | 73.2 | −48.1 | 118.2 | 9.5 | −37.3 | −6.0 | 118.2 | 57–196 |
| #40.1 | **−163.5** | −45.4 | −90.8 | −56.4 | 76.2 | −70.0 | −85.7 | 163.5 | 117–380 |

- **Gross grows, net does not.** The solver moves stock changes between legs (origins and exchange
  locations) about 2–3.6 times as much as in Z. In every year but 1995 the net stays within ±91 kt,
  close to Z's ±75 kt.
- **1995 is a sink year.** There is no net addition before 1996, so every stock leg's 1995 prior is
  the seed's zero. The solver draws −163.5 kt from them. In Z, BACI's 2 % share sat on those legs at
  +85.6 kt, and the posterior kept +77.4.
- **Mechanism.** BACI's 2 % now lands on Cathode→FU, which raises those priors by 1/0.98. Where the
  cathode trade exceeds what the region's supply and ICSG usage can absorb, the cheapest discharge is
  a stock leg priced by the seed's σ = max(0.01·|FU|, 0.3 kt) (ADR 0040).
