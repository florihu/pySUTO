## 1. Change

#70 solves cold periods concurrently on a thread pool of `N_WORKERS = os.cpu_count()` threads (8 here). Each
period pins Clarabel's linear solver (faer) to one thread. The base ran the periods one after another with
faer's default threading.

## 3. Per period and per phase

The script prints the full per-period table: status, objective, iterations, `F_opt` identity and
`solve_time_s`, for both runs. In summary:

- **Solve time per period:** 47.6–81.4 s in the base, 36.9–63.3 s in the new run. Σ `solve_time_s` is 1,873 s

## 5. Consequences

- **CLAUDE.md and the README** quote the measured runtime: about 12 min on copper with 8 cores, about 7 of
  them the probe.
- **The probe is the next lever.** Probing in parallel with the period solves, or skipping it on a constraint
  run already proven feasible, would save about 7 min. That is not part of #71.
- **Both runs stay on disk until #71 closes.** Deleting them afterwards is the maintainer's call.
