The cap is hitting on the **complementarity / centrality** side (`mu` ≈ 1e-13–1e-12, `ktratio` ≈ 1e-13), not on infeasibility. The solver is grinding around an optimum with poor centring, which is a near-degeneracy signature.

This is consistent with the regulariser term `‖(a−a₀)/σ_a‖²` dropping by ~10⁴ — a CV-from-0.001-to-0.1 jump scales σ_a by 100, so penalty mass divides by 10⁴ on every previously-pinned flat-prior edge.

## 4 — Where the σ_a = 1.0e-6 floor comes from

Walking the imputation → constraint chain:

1. [FlatPriorImputer](../src/pysuto/impute.py#L1309) (`step 5`, fills 460,423 / 854,460 edges = 54 %) computes
   ```python
   σ_j = CV[mask(j)] · scale(j)
   ```
   with `scale(j) = mean(|f_agg[i]|)` over the **(sector_from, entity_from, region_from, valuation)** group at flat-prior time. Per the impute docstring (line 1321): *"Empty group → σ_j = 0."*
2. The group `(Ore, Flow, ROW, Mass)` — and 30+ similar `(sector, Flow|Process, region, Mass/CC)` groups — has **zero observed |f_agg|** across all members (no feed ever touched any of them). Per the FlatPrior rule, **σ_j = 0** for every edge in such a group.
3. [`ConstraintRunBuilder`](../src/pysuto/constraints.py#L1107) (`sigma_a_abs_floor = 1e-6`) clamps the resulting zero to **1.0e-6**.

Verified directly: `agg_result.npz['sigma_agg']` has exactly **39,540 entries equal to 0** before clamping; in `constraints.npy:sigma_a` those same entries land at 1.0e-6. The breakdown by `(from_sector, valuation)`:

| from_sector | val | n pinned | from_sector | val | n pinned |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| Ore | Mass | 2,820 | CP | CC | 1,860 |
| MF | Mass | 2,820 | CP | Mass | 1,860 |
| MF | CC | 2,820 | SXEW | Mass | 1,800 |
| Smelting | Mass | 2,820 | SXEW | CC | 1,800 |
| ER | Mass | 2,820 | Ore | CC | 1,800 |
| CFR | Mass | 2,820 | CER | Mass | 1,410 |
| Scrap | Mass | 960 | CER | CC | 1,410 |
| Scrap | CC | 960 | CSXEW | Mass | 1,410 |
| Cathode | Mass | 990 | CSXEW | CC | 1,410 |
| CFR | CC | 1,140 | ER | CC | 1,380 |

(Total 41,129 with `σ_a < 1e-3`, of which 39,540 are at exactly 1e-6 after clamping.) Every entry has a₀ = 0.

**This is the structural root of the remaining infeasibility wall.** It was already responsible for the Ore-ROW pattern in the prior REP; it survived the `flat_prior_cv.csv` fix because that fix only changes the **CV**, not the **scale**, and CV × 0 still equals 0.

## 7 — Improvement recommendations (upstream)

### A — Fix the σ_a floor at its source

The σ_a = 1.0e-6 floor is the dominant remaining issue. It pins 41 k of 854 k edges (4.8 %) and is responsible for both the Ore-ROW wall (§2c) and the CP outlier (§2c).

1. **`FlatPriorImputer` should not return σ = 0 for empty groups.** When `scale(j) = mean(|f_agg|) = 0` because every member of `(sector_from, entity_from, region_from, valuation)` is itself flat-priored, fall back to a *broader* group (`sector_from, valuation`) or to a global median of |f_agg| over the *covered* universe. Two lines in [`_resolve_scale`](../src/pysuto/impute.py#L1431):
   ```python
   group_mean = np.where(counts > 0, sums / counts, 0.0)
   # NEW: broader fallback for empty groups
   global_scale = np.median(np.abs(f[np.isfinite(f) & (f != 0)]))
   group_mean = np.where(group_mean > 0, group_mean, global_scale)
   ```
   On the copper run this would set the four Ore\|ROW → MF/SXEW edges' σ to roughly `0.1 × median(|f|) ≈ 0.1 × O(1e2)` ≈ 10 t·Cu — enough for the solver to move them by 10⁵ when needed.

2. **Or: lift the `sigma_a_abs_floor` from 1e-6 to something meaningful.** 1e-6 is "thirty orders of magnitude below the scale of c". Set it to `1e-2 × global_median(|a₀|)` (or a per-sector typical scale). This is one constant in [`constraints.py:1107`](../src/pysuto/constraints.py#L1107).

Either (1) or (2) on its own should kill the z = 11.77 Ore wall outright.

### B — Re-tune the CV trade-off after (A)
