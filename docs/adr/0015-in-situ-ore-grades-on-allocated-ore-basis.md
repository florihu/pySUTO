# Ore grades are in-situ, on the price-allocated ore basis, region×year-resolved

**Status:** accepted.
Amended 2026-09-16 by [ADR 0028](0028-grade-sigma-is-stated-not-derived-from-the-band.md):
the grade rows' σ is now **stated** (`unc_type="sd"`) rather than derived from the band, so the
band's two jobs can move independently. The band values below are unchanged — a **year-local**
band was built and measured in Round M and rejected: it removed Chile's impossible 1.662 %
posterior ore grade but relocated the deletion (US ore 0.990 → 1.356) rather than reducing it. Evidence run: `opt_20260902_092933_02064ce2` (post mapped-self-trade); REP
of the grade-basis fix run under `docs/rep/`.

## Context

Three grade-related quantities exist per region-year, on two different bases, and until this ADR
the QP used none of the real data:

- the **crude_ore feed** carries S&P *price-allocated* ore mass (a poly-metallic mine's ore is
  split across its metals by price share — not physical ore; Chile 2015: 732,723 kt);
- the **S&P factor** (t ore per t metal) implies a *recovered-basis* grade `1/factor` (metal
  produced ÷ allocated ore; Chile 2015: 0.753 %) — the two are one dataset, so
  `grade × oreMass ≡ recovered production` holds to <1 % in every region checked;
- the model's **CO/Ore CC edges** are *in-situ* mill-feed copper, filled backward by the
  NetworkImputer as production ÷ recovery (Chile 2015: 3,993.7/0.8 + 1,778.4/0.725 = 7,445 kt ⇒
  implied grade 1.016 %).

The only grade coupling in the QP was the conc family's **global wildcard rows** (nominal 1.0 %,
hard band [0.3, 1.7] %): Chile's posterior grade drifted to 1.67–1.70 % against the accidental
global cap, Australia's allocated grade (1.81 %+) exceeded the cap from day one, and the
in-situ-vs-recovered gap (world 24.4 vs ~19 Mt Cu) fed a permanent σ-weighted tug.

## Decision

**The CO/Ore Mass↔CC coupling is owned by region×year `ore_grade` params on the in-situ,
allocated-ore basis**, generated by `scripts/build_ore_grade.py` from the registered icsg and
crude_ore feed runs' F_root (the build_trade_shares pattern):

    g_insitu(r,t) = [ConcSupply_CC(r,t)/eff_MF + SXEWSupply_CC(r,t)/eff_SXEW] / OreMass(r,t)

with eff nominals read from the eff params file (0.8 / 0.725 — the identity makes prior
consistency independent of their values) and `(1/factor)/eff_MF` as fallback where ICSG production
is absent (ROW = member mean). Hard band per region: its in-situ series min/max ∓/± 10 %. The conc
family's global CO/Ore rows are retired (skipped in `copper_params_parse.convert_conc`; removed
from `data/input/params/conc/current/params.csv`). One params file now drives the imputer twin map
(`conc_twin_map` overlay, most-specific row wins), the soft ratio rows, and the hard band — the
closest alignment yet with [ADR 0011](0011-one-edge-relation-shared-across-imputation-and-constraints.md)'s
one-relation-drives-all pattern.

## Consequences

- The triangle {OreMass feed} × {grade rows} ≡ {backward CC priors} holds **identically** at prior
  level, and the Ore-Mass twin (CC/g) now equals the crude_ore feed value exactly — the hard
  Mining Mass balance is satisfied at prior level and **WR ≈ 0 by construction**: under the
  allocated basis there is no separate waste-rock mass (this closes the June "crude_ore-vs-grade
  conflict"/negative-delta issue; the soft stripping-ratio row remains an inert free-cone row per
  that round's decision).
- Grades in this model are **allocated-basis in-situ** numbers, not physical mill-head grades
  (Australia ~2–3 % where mill-head is ~0.8 % — the allocation shrinks the denominator). Never
  compare them to literature head grades without converting.
- Coverage: region-years with ore mass but no grade source (~35, all tiny — Slovakia ≤421 kt ore
  over 20 y) have no Mass↔CC coupling; logged by the generator. The converse gap — production
  but no ore mass, i.e. every year past the crude_ore feed's end — is closed by carrying the
  grade forward ([ADR 0023](0023-ore-grade-carried-past-the-crude-ore-feed.md)).
- ⚠ `data/` is gitignored: on a fresh checkout run `scripts/build_ore_grade.py` (needs registered
  icsg + crude_ore feed runs) and re-apply the conc-file row removal by re-running
  `scripts/parse/copper_params_parse.py` semantics (the live conc file is hand-maintained — it
  carries ADR 0012's scrap band — so prefer deleting the three rows
  `conccosupply/concoreuse/concoresupply` directly).

## Considered options

- **Correct the grade param values but keep them unused** — no effect on the QP.
- **Layer region rows on top of the global 1 % rows** — double rows per edge, the global row keeps
  pulling every region toward 1 %.
- **Re-base the Ore CC edges to recovered basis** — breaks the recipe semantics (Tailings carry
  `(1−eff)` copper) and every downstream eff row.

## Amendment (2026-09-03): tailings conc is regionalised on the same basis

The global `conctailingsuse` row (nominal 0.55 %, band [0.1, 1] %) was the same disease one step
further: the imputer fills the tailings **Mass twin from that very nominal** (`network_mass_twin`
= CC/0.0055), while the **hard** MF/SXEW Mass balance implies a different tailings mass
(Ore_in − product). The mismatch cost 800k z² in `opt_20260903_084528_45fdd041` (largest single
ratio family; Panama z −84, US −69, Mexico −64).

The `ore_grade` artifact now also owns region×year×route tailings rows
(`oregradetailingsuse{mf|sxew}{region}{year}`, generated by the same
`scripts/build_ore_grade.py`), built from the prior's own construction so the twin closes the
process Mass balance at prior level (the WR≈0 trick):

    TailCC_route   = ProdCC · (1 − eff_route)/eff_route          (recipe residual)
    TailMass_route = (ProdCC/eff_route)/g_insitu − ProdCC/conc_product
    g_tail(route)  = TailCC_route / TailMass_route

with product conc nominals (Concentrate, CSXEW) read from the conc params file. Per-region
series bands ±10 %, as for the grade rows. The global `conctailingsuse` row is retired
(parse-script skip + live conc CSV row removal — conc v6, ore_grade v3); region-years without an
ICSG production source get no tailings row (honest flat-prior gap, as for CO/Ore). Slag stays
global (15k z², out of scope).
