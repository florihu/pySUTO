\section{Method}\label{sec:method}

Section~\ref{sec:framework} describes pySUTO, the generic framework, stage by
stage, each stage closing with what it does to the copper account;
Section~\ref{sec:application} gives the copper parametrisation and the
modelling decisions a reader needs in order to interpret the results;
Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} defines the quantities the Results report;
Section~\ref{sec:availability} states what is available for replication.

\subsection{The pySUTO framework}\label{sec:framework}

\subsubsection{Architecture}\label{sec:arch}

pySUTO organises the compilation and reconciliation of physical supply--use
tables into six sequential stages (Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline}): datafeed
assembly, aggregation and imputation, constraint building, per-year
constrained optimisation, posterior uncertainty propagation, and the
derivation of supply--use and input--output views. Each stage consumes the
persisted output of its predecessor and writes a registered run of its own,
and the quantity exchanged at every interface is the same: for each
\emph{edge} --- one directed flow between two nodes of the accounting graph,
resolved by period and valuation --- a value with its standard deviation
$\sigma$. No transformation may drop $\sigma$; that is what distinguishes
the framework from system-definition tools that do not reconcile
\citep{pauliuk2020odym} and from balancing tools that do not carry
per-source quality through \citep{lenzen2009matrix, wieland2022piolab}. The
copper graph has 1{,}081 nodes (23 sectors in 47 regions), two valuations and
30 years: 14{,}241 edges per valuation and year, 854{,}460 in all.

\subsubsection{Uncertainty treatment}\label{sec:uncertainty}

Uncertainty enters once, at the source. Each feed is scored on five
data-quality indicators --- reliability, completeness, and temporal,
geographical and other correlation --- on a four-point scale; the scores map
to coefficients of variation (CV) through the exponential model of
\citet{laner2016}, in the pedigree tradition of \citet{weidema1996pedigree},
and their quadrature sum is the feed's CV (SI~S2). Every later $\sigma$
operation instantiates one composition rule, the power-mean combiner
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{\oplus} \;=\; \Bigl(\sum\nolimits_k \lvert w_k\,
\sigma_k\rvert^{\,p}\Bigr)^{1/p},
\label{eq:lp}
\end{equation}
which merges weighted contributions $w_k \sigma_k$ into one standard
deviation. At $p=2$ it is the Gaussian quadrature of independent errors;
pySUTO uses $p=1.3$ throughout, which grants less diversification credit
because the contributions that meet on one edge --- parallel sources,
fanned-in sub-flows, chained imputations --- rarely err independently. For
$p \ge 1$ relative uncertainty never grows under mass-preserving aggregation
or disaggregation (SI~S2).

\subsubsection{Stage 1: Datafeed assembly}\label{sec:stage1}

Each source enters through its own concordance: raw records are cast as
directed source edges and projected onto the root accounting structure by a
sparse weight matrix $W$, $f_{\mathrm{root}} = W^{\top} f_{\mathrm{src}}$,
whose outgoing weights sum to one per source edge so that every fan-out
conserves mass (a guard rejects a projection off the matched raw total by
more than 1\%). A record that names only one endpoint is fanned across the
matching root edges by external split information --- for copper, bilateral
trade shares --- and never by a uniform $1/N$ split, which would fabricate
structure the source does not contain (ADR~0008). The feed's pedigree CV
attaches here, re-scored at the worst geographical level on edges touching
the rest-of-world aggregate (ADR~0003), and propagates through $W$ in CV form
by Eq.~\eqref{eq:lp} with the value shares of the contributing source edges
as weights: fan-in diversifies, fan-out leaves the CV unchanged. For copper,
three feeds leave Stage~1 (Table~\ref{tab:sources}): ICSG industry
statistics, scored $(2,1,1,1,1)$, CV~0.068 (0.217 on the 4{,}597
rest-of-world edges); BACI bilateral trade, $(3,2,1,1,2)$, CV~0.209 (0.294
on 9{,}148 edges); and crude-ore extraction, $(2,3,2,2,2)$, CV~0.104 (0.230
on 28 edges).

\subsubsection{Stage 2: Aggregation and imputation}\label{sec:stage2}

Where several feeds observe the same edge, their values are combined by
inverse variance, the aggregate's $\sigma$ following from Eq.~\eqref{eq:lp}
with the precision weights (SI~S3); a coverage mask records which edges any
feed observed. Bilateral trade legs then receive a size-damped $\sigma =
\mathrm{CV}\, a_{\mathrm{ref}}^{\,1-\gamma} a^{\gamma}$, $\gamma = 0.5$,
around a fixed per-product reference size, because a size-blind CV makes the
largest legs the cheapest to delete per tonne (ADR~0017). In the copper run
62{,}295 edges (7.29\%) are feed-observed: 24{,}787 in copper content from
ICSG, 36{,}643 in gross mass from BACI, 865 from the ore series.

The uncovered edges are filled by plugin imputers in two phases: a
\emph{cascade}, swept in fixed order until a full sweep fills no new edge,
and a single \emph{terminal} pass (rules in SI~S3). The cascade seeds
bilateral import legs in copper content from their observed gross-mass twin
($f_{\mathrm{CC}} = \mathit{conc} \cdot f_{\mathrm{Mass}}$), closes
process recipes --- a process's product equals its recovery efficiency times
its pooled inputs, secondary inputs and imports included, and its residual
output receives the complement --- and applies the concentration, ore-grade
and efficiency relations in reverse where only the downstream side is
observed, with a regularised sensitivity that damps the inversion of
near-zero coefficients. The terminal pass interpolates linearly in time on
within-region edges only, because a bilateral trade matrix is complete by
construction and a partner pair absent in one year is an observed zero, not
a gap (ADR~0026); seeds zero-mean stock-change priors scaled by observed
stock-change bands; and gives every still-empty edge a flat prior, value
zero with $\sigma = 10^{6}$, a noisy zero re-anchored in Stage~3. Fills are
write-once and logged with method, source edges and coefficients. In the
copper run the cascade converges in two passes; relational and temporal
imputation extend informative priors to 78{,}341 edges (9.17\%, mostly by
recipe closure and stock-change seeds; SI~S12), and 713{,}824 edges
(83.5\%) carry the flat prior.

\subsubsection{Stage 3: Constraint building}\label{sec:stage3}

The aggregated prior is next confronted with what must, and what should,
hold. Builder plugins emit rows of a sparse matrix $G$, each with its year,
target $c(t)$ and target uncertainty $\sigma_c(t)$: \emph{hard} rows must
hold exactly, \emph{soft} rows may be violated at a cost weighted by
$\sigma_c^{-2}$, in the $\sigma$-weighted-residual tradition of
accounting-matrix reconciliation \citep{byron1978, vanderploeg1982,
lenzen2009matrix} and of data reconciliation in material flow analysis
\citep{cencic2008stan}. The copper configuration registers, in build order
(SI~S4): hard mass balances per process and product node, region, year and
valuation; soft \emph{scale totals} that tie each node's total output to its
own aggregated prior --- self-anchored, adding no information but preserving
the prior's scale against homogeneous shrinkage (ADR~0006) --- with
$\sigma_c = \max(0.01\,|c|,\ 0.3\ \mathrm{kt})$ on feed-backed groups
(ADR~0025); soft level anchors on the observed scrap inputs of smelters and
refineries (ADR~0031); soft ratio rows for concentration, in-situ ore grade
and recovery efficiency, the last pooling all inputs of a process; hard
one-sided bands on the same ratios and on regional scrap shares; soft
zero-centred net-stock rows; and per-edge bounds $[l,u]$, stored but not
part of the optimisation. Prior $\sigma_a$ is then floored through
Eq.~\eqref{eq:lp}, and the noisy zeros are re-anchored: at $\sigma = 10^{6}$
their prior pull is nil, so the solver could route arbitrary mass through
data-free subgraphs; the anchor sets their $\sigma$ to 5\% of the median
prior magnitude among their sector--valuation siblings, exempting the
extraction chain and the residual outputs whose magnitude a relation
determines (ADR~0013). A build-time check verifies that no row couples
different years, so $G$ partitions into yearly blocks. For copper this
yields 1{,}365{,}461 rows, 45{,}416--45{,}584 per year: 903{,}502 hard
(39{,}480 mass balances, 864{,}022 bands) and 461{,}959 soft (27{,}128 scale
totals and scrap anchors, 434{,}831 ratio rows).

\subsubsection{Stage 4: Solver}\label{sec:stage4}

Each year is reconciled by one quadratic program over the flows $a$ and the
soft residuals $\varepsilon$,
\begin{equation}
\min_{a,\;\varepsilon}\;
(a - a_0)^{\top} \Sigma_a^{-1} (a - a_0)
\;+\; \varepsilon^{\top} \Sigma_c^{-1} \varepsilon
\quad \text{s.t.} \quad
G_s\, a - \varepsilon = c_s, \qquad
G_h\, a = c_h, \qquad
G_b\, a \le c_b,
\label{eq:qp}
\end{equation}
where $a_0$ is the prior, $\Sigma_a = \mathrm{diag}(\sigma_a^2)$ and
$\Sigma_c = \mathrm{diag}(\sigma_c^2)$ carry the accumulated uncertainties as
inverse-variance weights, and the rows of $G$ split into soft equalities
($G_s$), hard balances ($G_h$) and hard bands ($G_b$), with $c = c(t)$.
Clarabel, an interior-point solver for conic programs with quadratic
objectives \citep{goulart2024clarabel}, solves it with the three row groups
on two zero cones and one non-negative cone (SI~S5). Eq.~\eqref{eq:qp}
contains no bound constraints, by design: a data conflict should surface as
a visible residual, not vanish silently at a bound; the stored $[l,u]$ act
only in a pre-solve feasibility probe of the hard subsystem, which on
failure reports the most violated rows. The design has a cost the copper
results show: residual outputs in gross mass --- waste rock, tailings ---
carry neither anchor nor bound, so the solver may drive them negative to
relieve a mass balance. Over 1995--2024 the world waste-rock output has a
prior of $+292{,}395$~kt and a posterior of $-257{,}667$~kt, and 1{,}108
mass edges of the mine chain sit more than 1~kt below their lower bound
(issue~\#28). The shipped run uses the cold prior $a_0(t) =
f_{\mathrm{agg}}(t)$; all 30 copper years solve to full accuracy in 25--39
iterations and 5.8--8.5~s each, 188~s in all.

\subsubsection{Stage 5: Posterior uncertainty propagation}\label{sec:stage5}

The optimum $a^*$ is a point estimate; Stage~5 restores its distribution.
Modelling the prior as Gaussian with covariance $\Sigma_a$ and the
constraints as observations with covariance
$\Sigma_c^{\mathrm{full}} = \operatorname{blkdiag}(0, \Sigma_c)$ --- zero
variance on hard rows --- Gaussian conditioning gives the closed-form
posterior covariance
\begin{equation}
\Sigma_{a^*} \;=\; \Sigma_a \;-\; \Sigma_a G^{\top} S^{-1} G\, \Sigma_a,
\qquad S = G\, \Sigma_a G^{\top} + \Sigma_c^{\mathrm{full}},
\label{eq:posterior}
\end{equation}
the generalised-least-squares update of the reconciliation lineage
\citep{byron1978, vanderploeg1982}. Only the marginal variances are
extracted, by one sparse Cholesky factorisation of $S$ per year; intervals
$a^*_i \pm z\,\sigma_{a^*\!,i}$ are clipped to $[l_i, u_i]$ (SI~S6). Tests
enforce monotone shrinkage, positive semidefiniteness and the hard-only
limit of \citet{byron1978}. The stage has, however, not been executed on the
copper chain: the implementation assigns zero variance to every hard row,
including the 864{,}022 one-sided band rows, so $S$ has more zero-variance
rows per year than there are edges and is singular. An active-set treatment
--- only bands that bind at $a^*$ count as equalities --- is the required
amendment (issue~\#27); no posterior number is reported in this paper.

\subsubsection{Stage 6: Supply--use and input--output views}\label{sec:stage6}

Stage~6 materialises $a^*$ as a tidy physical supply--use table, every edge
of a period and valuation classified into a supply, use or final-use block
or a boundary block (extraction, secondary input, residual output);
aggregation over regions and sectors is a pushforward of that table that
preserves border crossings. The physical input--output table is a derived
view built on demand (ADR~0037): the market-share construct $D =
S\,\hat{q}^{-1}$ turns each use of a product into an edge between its
producer and its consumer \citep{millerblair2009}, and the
industry-by-industry calculus --- $Z$, $A = Z\hat{x}^{-1}$, $L = (I-A)^{-1}$,
footprints $e\,L\,\hat{y}$, embodied trade, economy-wide MFA indicators,
multipliers and structural decomposition --- follows \citet{wieland2022piolab}
and \citet{suh2004pio}, residual outputs being final outputs
\citep{altimirasmartin2014piot}. With one producer per product, as in
copper, this coincides with the product-by-product construct. Stage~6
propagates point estimates only.

\subsubsection{Implementation and provenance}\label{sec:software}

Every number above is reproducible from the repository. Inputs of four
kinds --- feeds, accounting structure, parameter tables and masks --- live
in a content-hashed registry: load-bearing files are SHA-256-hashed, any
change creates a new immutable version, and runs may pin old ones. Each stage
writes its outputs under a run identifier with a sidecar recording the exact
input versions, the upstream run identifiers, every configuration knob and
summary statistics, so that the chain from raw feed to table is
reconstructable from any run directory \citep{pauliuk2024traceability,
stodden2014best, wilkinson2016fair}. The package comprises 20{,}421 lines of
Python in 59 modules with 1{,}092 tests and four extension seams --- feeds,
imputers, constraint builders and posterior propagators subclass a common
interface \citep{pauliuk2015transparency, pauliuk2020cumulative}.

\subsection{Application to the global copper supply chain}\label{sec:application}

The framework is parametrised for copper by an accounting structure, three
data feeds with their pre-processing, six parameter tables and a set of
modelling decisions, each recorded in the repository as an architecture
decision record (ADR) with the measurement that motivated it; SI~S11 lists
all 37.

\subsubsection{System definition}\label{sec:system}

The account (Figure~\ref{fig:system}, SI~S8) has 23 sectors in each of 47
regions: seven processes from mining to a cathode pool, seven products from
ore to cathode, and nine boundary nodes --- crude ore in the ground on the
extraction side; waste rock, tailings, slag, scrap and producer stock change
on the socio-economic side; final use (semis fabrication) and user and
exchange stock changes as final demand. The 27 sector pairs of the process
chain define the edges. Four products are traded --- concentrate, matte,
anode and cathode --- so their six use pairs exist between every ordered
pair of regions; the other 21 pairs exist within a region only. Every edge
exists in gross mass (kt) and in contained copper (kt~CC), linked per product
by a concentration coefficient; only the three stock-change edges may be
negative. The 46 named regions cover every producer the ICSG reports
separately; a rest-of-world region absorbs 48 ICSG labels and 186 BACI
partner codes, among them ``Other Asia, not elsewhere specified'', so that
Taiwan is a named region without trade data. The period is 1995--2024.

\subsubsection{Data sources}\label{sec:sources}

Three sources feed the account (Table~\ref{tab:sources}). Industry
statistics come from four editions of the ICSG yearbook
\citep{icsg2005yearbook, icsg2014yearbook, icsg2023yearbook,
icsg2025yearbook}, combined newest-wins where they overlap: mine, smelter
and refinery production, refined usage, secondary feed and stock changes, in
copper content. Bilateral trade comes from BACI HS92, release V202601
\citep{gaulier2010baci, cepii2026baci}, for concentrate (HS~260300), matte
(740110, 740120), anode (740200) and cathode (740311), in gross mass, on
cross-region edges only (ADR~0014). Crude-ore extraction comes from the
materialflows.net series \citep{kroiss2024crudeore}, a price-allocated ore

\subsubsection{Industry statistics: parsing and pre-processing}\label{sec:icsg}

Three defects of the yearbooks were found by the reconciliation and fixed at
the input, never by loosening a constraint (SI~S9). In the 2014 edition the
country name sits on the middle row of a block, so a naive parse handed each
block's total to the next country: 16 rows and 11{,}558~kt~CC of mine
production that never existed (ADR~0035). A type renamed between editions is
a second stream, not a restatement; summing instead of deduplicating
restored 1{,}038~kt of Zambian primary smelting in 2004--07 (ADR~0036). And
one territory carries one label before any region-keyed step (ADR~0032).
Production is then converted into the input it consumed, because the account
books flows into a process: smelter output divided by the smelting recovery
becomes concentrate and scrap into smelting, refined output divided by the
electrolytic recovery becomes anode into refining (ADR~0021). The secondary
series required care: secondary refined production already contains the
copper that came through secondary smelters, and booking both ICSG series
counted 70~Mt of scrap twice over 30 years; direct-to-refinery scrap is
therefore $\max\bigl(0,\, S_{\mathrm{ref}} -
S_{\mathrm{sm}}\,\eta_{\mathrm{CFR}} \eta_{\mathrm{ER}}\bigr)/
(\eta_{\mathrm{CFR}}\eta_{\mathrm{ER}})$, so secondary copper enters the
chain once (ADR~0030). Finally, ICSG usage names only the consumer; each
such record is fanned across supplying regions by trade shares built from
the netted BACI matrix, with the domestic leg capped at the region's own
production (ADR~0024).

\subsubsection{Trade statistics: reconstruction and re-export netting}\label{sec:baci}

Two defects of the trade matrix are corrected before it enters. First,
BACI's quantity field contradicts its own value field on a set of
concentrate legs: with the cathode unit value of the same release as the
copper price, the value-implied copper fraction exceeds the physical band.
Where both the leg and the exporter-year imply more than 35\% copper and the
leg is at least 1~kt, the quantity is rebuilt from the intact value, $q =
v/(P_{\mathrm{Cu}}\, g_{\mathrm{ref}})$, and the inference is carried in
$\sigma$: 881 of 18{,}141 legs, 54 exporters, 82{,}320~kt of export mass over
30 years, 58{,}517 of them Chilean (ADR~0033). Second, bilateral statistics
record gross border crossings, so a consignment re-exported unchanged
through a warehouse economy is counted twice, and the second crossing asks
the account for production the hub does not have. For each product, hub and
year we read imports $I$ and exports $X$ from the trade matrix and intake $U$
and production $P$ from the industry statistics, all in copper content, and
net the re-exported volume
\begin{equation}
T = \min\bigl(X,\, I,\, \max(R, E)\bigr), \qquad
R = \max(0,\, I-U), \qquad
E = \max(0,\, X-P),
\label{eq:reexport}
\end{equation}
where $R$ is the surplus-import reading of the same physical fact and $E$
the uncovered-export reading, domestic production being taken to supply
exports first. $T$ is moved from the hub's legs to the direct
origin--destination legs in proportion to the observed inbound and outbound
shares; re-imports are dropped, the rest-of-world aggregate is never a hub,
and the caps $T \le X$ and $T \le I$ leave producers with thin industry rows
untouched. Over 1995--2024 the rule moves 11{,}028~kt~CC; arrivals without
a recorded outbound leg stay in a residual ledger for the reconciliation to
judge (ADR~0027, SI~S9).

\subsubsection{Ore grade and feed build order}\label{sec:grade}

Ore is coupled to concentrate through a region-by-year in-situ grade, $g =
(\mathrm{Conc}_{\mathrm{CC}}/\eta_{\mathrm{MF}} +
\mathrm{SXEW}_{\mathrm{CC}}/\eta_{\mathrm{SXEW}})$ over the allocated ore
mass, stated for 887 region-years in 35 mining regions (median 1.12\%,
10th--90th percentiles 0.61--2.47\%); its hard band is the region's series
envelope widened by 10\%, and its $\sigma$ is stated separately from the
band (ADR~0028). The feeds are built in a fixed order
(Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline}): parse, re-export netting, the BACI feed, the
trade shares, the ICSG feed, the ore grades, then Stage~2. The order is
load-bearing --- the trade shares read the netted matrix and the ICSG feed's
own production --- and nothing enforces it; one skipped rebuild cost 6.2
percentage points of objective. The shipped chain itself carries two stale
derived inputs, an ore-grade table and a netted matrix built before the last
two yearbook corrections, which the next rebuild replaces (issue~\#30).

\subsubsection{Parameters}\label{sec:params}

Six parameter tables carry the physics (SI~S10). Concentrations are global:
concentrate 0.275 with $\sigma$ 0.0375 and hard band $[0.20, 0.35]$, matte
0.525 $[0.30, 0.75]$, anode 0.94 $[0.90, 0.98]$, cathodes 0.9975
$[0.995, 1.0]$, slag 0.0275, and scrap as one blended commodity, 0.70 in
$[0.40, 0.98]$, because two grade classes on one node would give the solver
a free switch (ADR~0012). Recovery efficiencies are the medians of the
literature ranges \citep{schlesinger2022extractive,
davenport2002extractive}: flotation 0.80 $[0.70, 0.90]$, leaching 0.725
$[0.60, 0.85]$, smelting 0.965 $[0.95, 0.98]$, converting and fire refining
0.9875 $[0.98, 0.995]$ and electrolytic refining 0.997 $[0.995, 0.999]$. The
last three are \emph{pooled}: a smelter's ratio row relates its matte to
concentrate and scrap from every origin, a refinery's relates its cathode to
anode from every source region (ADR~0005, 0022). Before refining was pooled,
imported anode was not an input of any recipe and the solver deleted 96\% of
the observed anode trade rather than violate the node; pooling cut the
scale-total objective by 26.4\% in one step, the largest single improvement
of the study. Per-region scrap-share bands are the 5th--95th percentiles of
the ICSG share widened by 0.05; net-stock rows are bounded at the 95th
percentile of observed stock change over throughput (USC 2.4\%, PSC 5.0\%,
MESC 10.1\%; 910\% for the exchange hubs Netherlands, Singapore and United
Kingdom). One property of the $\sigma$ scaling must be stated: the soft
ratio rows read the parameter $\sigma$, given in ratio units, as a relative
$\sigma$, so the concentrate row is weighted at 3.75\% rather than the
13.6\% the table implies (issue~\#29).

\subsubsection{Critical modelling choices}\label{sec:choices}

Table~\ref{tab:choices} collects the choices a reader needs in order to
interpret the results, each with the alternative that was measured and
rejected. Three deserve a sentence. Scale totals are self-anchored, not
external control totals: the yearbook figures enter as edge observations with
their pedigree $\sigma$, and no total outranks them (ADR~0006); their 0.3~kt
floor applies to feed-backed totals only, because a global floor let
fabricated small chains grow and cut Chile's concentrate export retention by
8 percentage points (ADR~0025). $\sigma$ is never loosened to repair a
contradiction between sources: loosening the fabricated totals collapsed
world ore retention from 0.66 to 0.41, and loosening contradicted nodes
repriced the disagreement while 0.46\% of the mass moved (ADR~0016, 0019);
the input is fixed instead. And the concentrate grade stays global, because
the between-region signal (sd 0.0117) is a third of the $\sigma$ the single
row already carries, and half of the deletion a regional grade was meant to
relieve is forced by missing export mass, which no grade can repair
(ADR~0029).

\subsubsection{Limitations of the application}\label{sec:limits}

Six limitations stand. Taiwan carries no trade because BACI folds it into an
aggregate partner, so its usage records (15.9~Mt~CC over the period) stay
undistributed. Mine output is booked in five countries that mine no copper
(845~kt~CC over the period), an artefact of recipe closure on refined output.
Where two ICSG anchors disagree, the solver pays with trade and ore, because
a trade leg's CV (0.069 at the median leg) exceeds an anchor's (0.017).
Stage~5 has not run (issue~\#27), the residual mass edges act as signed
slack (issue~\#28), the ratio-row $\sigma$ scaling departs from the tables
(issue~\#29), and two derived inputs are stale (issue~\#30). Stage~6
carries point estimates only.

\subsection{Evaluation}\label{sec:evaluation}

Each run is judged with a fixed set of quantities (formulas in SI~S13). The
\emph{defect} of an edge is $a^* - a_{\mathrm{prior}}$, standardised as $z =
(a^* - a_{\mathrm{prior}})/\sigma_a$; the \emph{residual} of a soft row is
its slack $\varepsilon$, standardised as $z = \varepsilon/\sigma_c$. The
objective of Eq.~\eqref{eq:qp} is $\sum z^2$ over edges and soft rows, so an
item's \emph{objective share} $z^2/\sum z^2$ within its family --- edge
defects, scale-total residuals, soft-ratio residuals --- is what it cost the
optimiser; the ranking of identities by share across years is the primary
defect diagnostic. \emph{Retention} is $\sum a^*/\sum a_{\mathrm{prior}}$
over the edges of one category in one valuation and year: 1 keeps the prior
scale, below 1 is deletion. Deletion at a mine node splits into a
\emph{forced} part, $\sum \max(0,\, P - 0.35X - U)$, which no optimiser
could avoid because copper leaves the node only as domestic intake $U$ or as
export mass $X$ at a grade capped at 0.35, and a \emph{discretionary}
remainder that $\sigma$ and anchoring can move. Runs are compared on a
\emph{common set}, $\sum z^2$ over the scale-total rows present in every run
compared, over all 30 years. Every change is one round, one ADR and one
report, measured against its predecessor and the base run of the study; a
$\sigma$ or repricing change ships only under a stop rule --- objective by
anchor band, mass moved, world retention of ore, concentrate and cathode not
falling by more than 0.01, the posterior-to-prior mass ratio of small nodes
within 0.05, and the concentrate export retention of Chile, Peru and
Indonesia within 0.01. On the shipped run the common-set objective is
1{,}552{,}144, 9.6\% below the base; 24{,}884~kt~CC of mine output is
deleted over 30 years, 3{,}013 of them (12.1\%) forced; world concentrate
retention is 0.938; and 132 region-years sit on the export-grade cap.

pySUTO is developed at \url{https://github.com/florihu/pySUTO} and will be
archived with a DOI at Zenodo at submission [DOI and licence to be

three feed runs, the imputation, constraint and optimisation runs with their
sidecars, priors, $\sigma$, constraint tables and results --- so that every
number in this paper can be recomputed or traced to a file. BACI is open

allocation are licensed and are not redistributed, but the published parse
scripts rebuild the feeds from the originals. The scrap-share, stock-change
and ore-grade tables are statistics derived from those sources, and their
release is subject to the same licence check. The study uses published
statistics only and involved no human participants.
