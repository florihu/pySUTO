\section{Method}\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Architecture}\label{sec:arch}

pySUTO organises the compilation and reconciliation of physical supply--use
tables into five sequential stages (Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline}): datafeed
assembly, aggregation and imputation, constraint building, constrained
optimisation, and posterior uncertainty propagation. Each stage consumes the
persisted output of its predecessor and writes a registered run of its own,
and the quantity exchanged at every interface is the same: for each
\emph{edge} --- one directed flow between two nodes of the accounting graph,
resolved by period and valuation --- a value together with its standard
deviation $\sigma$. Stage~1 runs once per source; all later stages operate on
the combined edge set. In the copper application, three feeds populate
28{,}482 edges per year across 47 regions and two valuations over 1995--2024.

This design joins two strands that industrial ecology software has kept
separate. System-definition frameworks such as ODYM
\citep{pauliuk2020odym} and the general socioeconomic-metabolism data model
\citep{pauliuk2019datamodel} structure material-system data but do not
reconcile it; the constrained-balancing lineage of AISHA, large-scale matrix
balancing, and the PIOLab \citep{geschke2011aisha, lenzen2009matrix,
wieland2022piolab} reconciles large accounts but does not carry per-source
data quality through to the result. pySUTO does both by treating $\sigma$ as
a first-class artifact of every stage.

\subsection{Uncertainty treatment}\label{sec:uncertainty}

Uncertainty enters once, at the source, and is then only transformed. Each
feed is scored on five data-quality indicators --- reliability, completeness,
and temporal, geographical, and other correlation --- and the scores are
translated into per-indicator coefficients of variation (CVs) with the
exponential model of \citet{laner2016}, in the pedigree tradition of
\citet{weidema1996pedigree}; their quadrature sum is the feed's CV
(coefficients in Supporting Information~S1). From this point, no
transformation may drop $\sigma$.

All subsequent $\sigma$ arithmetic instantiates a single composition rule,
the power-mean combiner
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{\oplus} \;=\; \Bigl(\sum\nolimits_k \lvert w_k\,
\sigma_k\rvert^{\,p}\Bigr)^{1/p},
\label{eq:lp}
\end{equation}
which merges weighted contributions $w_k \sigma_k$ into one standard
deviation. At $p=2$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:lp} is the Gaussian quadrature of
independent errors; pySUTO's default $p=1.3$ (configurable) deliberately
grants less diversification credit, because the many contributions that meet
on one edge --- parallel sources, fanned-in sub-flows --- rarely err
independently. For $p \ge 1$ the rule preserves a useful invariant: relative
uncertainty never grows under mass-preserving aggregation or disaggregation.
The stage descriptions below state where Eq.~\eqref{eq:lp} fires and with
which weights; Stage~5 then converts the accumulated prior $\sigma$ into
posterior uncertainty.

\subsection{Stage 1: Datafeed assembly}\label{sec:stage1}

Each source enters through its own concordance. Raw records are first cast as
directed source edges, then projected onto the root accounting structure by a
sparse weight matrix $W$, $f_{\mathrm{root}} = W^{\top} f_{\mathrm{src}}$,
whose outgoing weights sum to one per source edge so that every fan-out
conserves mass. Where the split of an aggregate onto root edges is known --- for example,
from bilateral trade shares --- those shares override the uniform weights. A fan with no split information is not
distributed at all: a uniform $1/N$ allocation would fabricate bilateral
structure the source does not contain, so the record is left undistributed
and its information enters later as a constraint. The projection is guarded
--- total mass must be conserved to within 1\%, and a raw record fanning
onto several root edges is counted once, never replicated.

One source is adjusted before it enters. Bilateral trade statistics record
gross border crossings, so a consignment that is imported into a warehouse or
merchanting economy and re-exported unchanged is counted twice, and the second
crossing asks the accounts for domestic production the exporting region does
not have. We re-attribute that tonnage at feed time. For each product,
region and year we read four observed quantities in copper content ---
imports $I$ and exports $X$ from the trade matrix, intake $U$ and production
$P$ from the industry statistics --- and net the re-exported volume
\begin{equation}
T = \min\bigl(X,\, I,\, \max(R, E)\bigr), \qquad
R = \max(0,\, I-U), \qquad
E = \max(0,\, X-P),
\label{eq:reexport}
\end{equation}
where $R$ is the surplus-import reading of the same physical fact (what the
region imported and did not use) and $E$ the uncovered-export reading (what it
exported and could not have produced), domestic production being taken to
supply exports first. The volume is
removed from both of the hub's legs and added to the direct origin--destination
legs in proportion to the observed inbound and outbound shares; a pair whose
two ends coincide is a re-import and is dropped. The caps $T \le X$ and
$T \le I$ make the rule inert for producers with thin industry rows, which
are never netted. Adjusted legs carry a compounded uncertainty,
$\sigma_{\mathrm{new}} = \mathrm{CV}\sqrt{v_{\mathrm{obs}}^{2} +
2\Delta^{2}}$, floored so that no netting step can make a leg more certain
than its pedigree. Tonnage that arrives at a region and has no recorded
outbound leg cannot be re-attributed from trade data alone; it is retained,
labelled in a residual ledger, and left for the reconciliation to judge.

The feed's pedigree CV (Section~\ref{sec:uncertainty}) attaches at this
point, with one penalty: edges touching an aggregate rest-of-world region are
re-scored at the worst geographical-correlation level, since such composites
inherit the weakest regional attribution.

Uncertainty then propagates through $W$ in CV form, by
Eq.~\eqref{eq:lp} with the value shares $\omega_{ij}$ of the contributing
source edges as weights:
$\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{root},j} = \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{src}}
(\sum_i \omega_{ij}^{\,p})^{1/p}$. Because the shares sum to one, fan-in
diversifies ($\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{root}} \le \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{src}}$ for
$p \ge 1$) while fan-out leaves the CV unchanged. Stage~1 ends with one
root-aligned pair $(F_k, \sigma_k)$ per feed $k$ in the feed registry ---
for copper: industry statistics reported in metal content, bilateral trade
in gross mass net of re-exports, and national crude-ore extraction.

\subsection{Stage 2: Aggregation and imputation}\label{sec:stage2}

Where several feeds observe the same edge, their values are combined by
inverse variance: weights $\Omega_{kj} = \sigma_{kj}^{-2} / \sum_k
\sigma_{kj}^{-2}$ give the aggregate $f_{\mathrm{agg},j} = \sum_k \Omega_{kj}
F_{kj}$, and its $\sigma_{\mathrm{agg}}$ follows from Eq.~\eqref{eq:lp} with
weights $\Omega_{kj}$. At $p=2$ this is the classical minimum-variance
combination of independent estimates; the default $p=1.3$ keeps the aggregate
deliberately more cautious. A coverage mask records which edges any feed
observed.

The uncovered edges are filled by an imputation engine that runs plugin
imputers in two phases: a \emph{cascade}, swept repeatedly in fixed order
until a full sweep fills no new edge, and a single \emph{terminal} pass. The
cascade seeds bilateral trade legs, closes process recipes, applies parameter
relations, and evaluates formula splits. Fills are write-once, and every fill is logged
with its method, source edges, and coefficients --- the imputation provenance
of each edge.

Two cascade members do most of the work. The trade imputer converts observed
gross-mass trade into metal content through the concentration relation
$f_{\mathrm{CC}} = \mathit{conc} \cdot f_{\mathrm{Mass}}$. The network
imputer traverses process recipes: a process's product equals its recovery
efficiency times its pooled inputs (secondary inputs and imports included),
its residual output receives the complement $(1-\mathit{eff})$, and known
totals propagate to the single missing edge of a node balance; each filled
content edge also seeds its mass twin. The parametric imputers apply the same
relations in reverse where only the downstream side is observed, damping the
inversion of near-zero coefficients (an ore grade of $0.01$ would otherwise
amplify $\sigma$ a hundredfold) with a regularised sensitivity
(Supporting Information~S2). All imputed $\sigma$ compose via
Eq.~\eqref{eq:lp}.

The terminal pass interpolates remaining gaps in time, with $\sigma$ inflated
per gap year; seeds zero-mean stock-change priors scaled by observed
stock-change bands; and assigns every still-empty edge a flat prior --- value
zero with $\sigma = 10^{6}$, a noisy zero re-anchored in Stage~3
(a magnitude-aware flat-prior mode is available as an alternative). Temporal
interpolation is scoped to within-region edges. A bilateral trade matrix is
complete by construction, so a partner pair absent in one year is an observed
zero and not a gap; interpolating it would manufacture trade the source
denies, and the missing route is left to the flat prior instead. In the
copper application the cascade converges in two passes: 7.3\% of the
854{,}460 edges are feed-observed, relational and temporal imputation extend
informative priors to a further 9.1\%, and the remaining 83.6\% carry flat
priors.

\subsection{Stage 3: Constraint building}\label{sec:stage3}

The aggregated prior is next confronted with what must, and what should, hold.
Constraint builders --- plugins like the imputers --- emit rows of a sparse
matrix $G$, each row carrying its year, its target $c(t)$, and a target
uncertainty $\sigma_c(t)$. \emph{Hard} rows must hold exactly; \emph{soft}
rows may be violated at a cost weighted by $\sigma_c^{-2}$, in the
$\sigma$-weighted-residual tradition of accounting-matrix reconciliation
\citep{byron1978, vanderploeg1982, lenzen2009matrix} and of data
reconciliation in material flow analysis \citep{cencic2008stan}.

The reference configuration registers five families. Hard mass balances
enforce supply\,$=$\,use per node, region, year, and valuation, with
system-boundary nodes exempt. Soft \emph{scale totals} tie each total-output
group to its own aggregated sum ($\sigma_c = 0.01\,|c|$); their targets are
self-anchored, adding no external information but preserving the prior's
scale against homogeneous shrinkage of the solution. Soft ratio rows impose
concentration and recovery-efficiency relations (efficiency rows pool all
inputs of a process), their parameter uncertainty rescaled to flow space by a
Gauss--Newton linearisation at the prior; zero-centred net-stock rows bound
stock changes within observed bands. Hard one-sided band rows cap
concentration, efficiency, and scrap-share ratios. Per-edge bounds $[l,u]$
are stored but are not part of the optimisation
(Section~\ref{sec:stage4}). For copper this yields ${\approx}45{,}900$ rows
per year, 1.38 million in total.

Two conditioning steps close the stage. Prior $\sigma_a$ is floored through
Eq.~\eqref{eq:lp} with a relative and an absolute term, and edges that arrive
as noisy zeros are re-anchored: at $\sigma = 10^{6}$ their prior pull is
${\approx}\,0$, so the solver could route arbitrary mass through data-free
subgraphs. The anchor sets their $\sigma$ to 5\% of the median prior
magnitude among their sector--valuation siblings --- a soft pull toward zero
at a sector-appropriate scale --- exempting sectors whose magnitude is
structurally determined by a ratio or balance relation rather than by data.
A final check verifies that no row couples different years, so $G$ partitions
into independent per-year blocks.

\subsection{Stage 4: Solver}\label{sec:stage4}

Each year is reconciled by a quadratic program over the flows $a$ and the
soft residuals $\varepsilon$,
\begin{equation}
\min_{a,\;\varepsilon}\;
(a - a_0)^{\top} \Sigma_a^{-1} (a - a_0)
\;+\; \varepsilon^{\top} \Sigma_c^{-1} \varepsilon
\quad \text{s.t.} \quad
G_s\, a - \varepsilon = c_s, \qquad
G_h\, a = c_h, \qquad
G_b\, a \le c_b,
\label{eq:qp}
\end{equation}
where $a_0$ is the prior, $\Sigma_a = \mathrm{diag}(\sigma_a^2)$ and
$\Sigma_c = \mathrm{diag}(\sigma_c^2)$ carry the accumulated uncertainties as
inverse-variance weights, and the rows of $G$ split into soft equalities
($G_s$), hard balances ($G_h$), and hard bands ($G_b$), with $c = c(t)$.
The problem is solved by Clarabel, an interior-point solver for conic
programs with quadratic objectives \citep{goulart2024clarabel}; the three row
groups map onto two zero cones and one nonnegative cone (Supporting
Information~S4).

Eq.~\eqref{eq:qp} contains no bound constraints. Non-negativity is induced
rather than imposed --- by the zero-anchored priors and the hard bands ---
so that a data conflict surfaces as a visible residual instead of being
absorbed silently at a bound. The stored $[l,u]$ act only twice: in a
pre-solve feasibility probe of the hard subsystem, which on failure reports
the most violated rows and thereby turns infeasibility into a data
diagnostic, and in the interval clipping of Stage~5.

Before each solve, $\sigma_a$ is clipped to a fixed dynamic range by a
conditioning rule shared with Stage~5, so that solver and posterior see the
same effective prior. The year blocks solve independently (for copper,
28{,}482 edges by ${\approx}45{,}900$ rows each, in place of one monolithic
system). The executed default is a cold prior,
$a_0(t) = f_{\mathrm{agg}}(t)$; chaining
the previous optimum, $a_0(t) = a^*(t{-}1)$, is available as a warm
alternative. Independently of the prior choice, the solver's KKT
factorisation is reused across years whose block structure is identical.
Each solve returns the reconciled flows $a^*(t)$, the residuals
$\varepsilon^*(t)$, and per-row diagnostics.

\subsection{Stage 5: Posterior uncertainty propagation}\label{sec:stage5}

The optimum $a^*$ is a point estimate; Stage~5 restores its distribution.
Modelling the prior as Gaussian with covariance $\Sigma_a$ and the
constraints as observations with covariance
$\Sigma_c^{\mathrm{full}} = \operatorname{blkdiag}(0, \Sigma_c)$ --- zero
variance on hard rows --- Gaussian conditioning gives the closed-form
posterior covariance
\begin{equation}
\Sigma_{a^*} \;=\; \Sigma_a \;-\; \Sigma_a G^{\top} S^{-1} G\, \Sigma_a,
\qquad S = G\, \Sigma_a G^{\top} + \Sigma_c^{\mathrm{full}},
\label{eq:posterior}
\end{equation}
the generalised-least-squares update of the reconciliation lineage
\citep{byron1978, vanderploeg1982}. Only the marginal variances are
extracted, via one sparse Cholesky factorisation $S = LL^{\top}$ per year
with $\sigma^2_{a^*\!,i} = \sigma^2_{a,i}\,(1 - \sigma^2_{a,i}
\|L^{-1} g_i\|_2^2)$; intervals $a^*_i \pm z\,\sigma_{a^*\!,i}$ are clipped
to $[l_i, u_i]$. Hard-balanced edges obtain zero-width intervals --- the
correct limit, not an artefact. Three invariants are enforced by tests:
posterior variance never exceeds prior variance, the update stays positive
semidefinite, and as $\Sigma_c \to 0$ it recovers the hard-constraints-only
reduction of \citet{byron1978}. Two qualifications remain: the update is the
linear-Gaussian approximation of the true posterior, and it is blind to the
bounds, so $\sigma_{a^*}$ overestimates uncertainty where the solution rests
on them. The posterior thereby makes explicit which parts of the account are
fixed by structure and which remain limited by data --- the information a
point estimate alone cannot convey.

\subsubsection{Software architecture and reproducibility}\label{sec:software}

Every number above is reproducible from the repository. Inputs live in a
content-hashed registry: load-bearing files are SHA-256-hashed, any change
creates a new immutable version, and runs may pin old ones. Each stage writes
its outputs under a timestamped run identifier with a sidecar recording the
exact input versions, the upstream run identifiers, and every configuration
knob --- a provenance chain from raw feed to posterior interval that is
enforced, not merely recorded: a stage refuses inputs built against a
different accounting-structure version \citep{pauliuk2024traceability,
stodden2014best, wilkinson2016fair}.

The package comprises ${\sim}19{,}000$ lines of Python in 60 modules with 839
tests, strictly layered imports (domain $\to$ pipeline $\to$ diagnostics),
and two extension seams --- imputers and constraint builders subclass a
common interface --- following the field's call for transparent, reusable
model software \citep{pauliuk2015transparency, pauliuk2020cumulative}. It builds on the scientific
Python stack and Clarabel, with an optional sparse-Cholesky accelerator for
Stage~5.
