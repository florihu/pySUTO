This Supporting Information states the complete mathematical rules of the five
pySUTO stages summarised in the Method section of the main text. Section
numbers mirror the pipeline: notation (\S\ref{si:notation}), datafeed assembly
(\S\ref{si:stage1}), aggregation and imputation (\S\ref{si:stage2}), constraint
building (\S\ref{si:stage3}), the solver (\S\ref{si:stage4}), posterior
uncertainty propagation (\S\ref{si:stage5}), and the provenance schema
(\S\ref{si:provenance}). Numerical values are the reference-configuration
defaults recorded in the run sidecars; application-specific parameter values
(concentrations, efficiencies, bands) belong to the application dataset, not to
the framework.

\section{Stage 1 --- Datafeed assembly}\label{si:stage1}
Implemented in \texttt{pysuto/\{unc,conc,feedpipe\}.py}.

\subsection{Pedigree scores to coefficients of variation}
Each feed carries one pedigree score $s\in\{1,2,3,4\}$ per data-quality
indicator (reliability, completeness, temporal, geographical, and other
correlation), following \citet{laner2016} with the pedigree lineage of
\citet{weidema1996pedigree}. Scores map to per-indicator coefficients of
variation through the exponential model
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{rel}} = \frac{0.75\, e^{1.105\, s_{\mathrm{rel}}}}{100},
\qquad
\mathrm{CV}_{i} =
\begin{cases}
0 & s_i = 1,\\[2pt]
\dfrac{a_i\, e^{1.105\,(s_i-1)}}{100} & s_i > 1,
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
where the sensitivity coefficient $a_i$ is $0.375$ (not sensitive), $0.750$
(medium), or $1.5$ (highly sensitive) depending on the indicator's assessed
influence. The indicators combine in quadrature to one per-feed coefficient of
variation,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}} = \sqrt{\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{rel}}^2 +
\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{comp}}^2 + \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{temp}}^2 +
\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{geo}}^2 + \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{oth}}^2}.
\end{equation}
Edges touching an aggregate rest-of-world region are penalised by the factor
$\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}(s_{\mathrm{geo}}{=}4)/\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}(s_{\mathrm{geo}})$,
equivalent to re-scoring them at the worst geographical correlation.

\subsection{Concordance}
Raw records are first cast as directed source edges, then mapped onto the root
accounting structure by a sparse weight matrix $W$:
\begin{equation}
f_{\mathrm{root}} = W^{\top} f_{\mathrm{src}},
\qquad \textstyle\sum_j W_{ij} = 1 \ \text{for every source edge } i,
\end{equation}
so every fan-out conserves mass. Uniform weights are overridden where external
split information exists (in the copper application, bilateral trade shares).
A fan with no split information is \emph{not} distributed: asserting a uniform
$1/N$ partner pattern would fabricate structure the source does not contain,
so such rows remain undistributed and their information enters only through
constraints.

The bilateral trade matrix that supplies those shares is netted of re-exports
before it enters (Eq.~\ref{eq:reexport}): tonnage that a region imported and
re-exported unchanged is removed from both of its legs and added to the direct
origin--destination leg in proportion to the observed inbound and outbound
shares. The adjustment is bounded by the region's own recorded imports and
exports, so a producer whose industry rows are thin is never netted, and a
re-attributed pair whose two ends fold to the same root region is a re-import
and is dropped with the other mapped self-trade. Adjusted legs carry a
compounded standard deviation and inferred legs a $\sqrt{2}$ pedigree factor,
floored so that netting never makes a leg more certain than its source.

\subsection{CV-invariant propagation}
Uncertainty propagates through $W$ in coefficient-of-variation form. With
value shares $A_{ij} = |W_{ij} f_{\mathrm{src},i}|$ and fan-in weights
$\omega_{ij} = A_{ij}/\sum_k A_{kj}$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{root},j} = \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}
\Bigl(\sum_i \omega_{ij}^{\,p}\Bigr)^{1/p},
\qquad
\sigma_{\mathrm{root},j} = \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{root},j}\,
|f_{\mathrm{root},j}|.
\label{eq:si-cvprop}
\end{equation}
Because the $\omega_{ij}$ lie on the simplex, $\omega_{ij}^{\,p} \le
\omega_{ij}$ for $p \ge 1$ and hence $\sum_i \omega_{ij}^{\,p} \le 1$: fan-in
diversifies ($\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{root}} \le \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}$),
pure fan-out leaves the CV unchanged, and no concordance operation can
increase relative uncertainty. Stage 1 outputs one root-aligned pair
$(F_k, \sigma_k)$ per feed.

\section{Stage 2 --- Aggregation and imputation}\label{si:stage2}
Implemented in \texttt{pysuto/\{collector,agg\}.py} and \texttt{pysuto/impute/}.

\subsection{Inverse-variance aggregation}
With per-feed precisions $P_{kj} = \sigma_{kj}^{-2}$ (zero where feed $k$ does
not cover edge $j$; observations below $0.01$\,kt are suppressed as noise),
\begin{equation}
\Omega_{kj} = \frac{P_{kj}}{\sum_k P_{kj}}, \qquad
f_{\mathrm{agg},j} = \sum_k \Omega_{kj} F_{kj}, \qquad
\sigma_{\mathrm{agg},j} = \Lp{\{\Omega_{kj}\sigma_{kj}\}_k}.
\label{eq:si-agg}
\end{equation}
At $p=2$, $\sigma_{\mathrm{agg},j} = (\sum_k \sigma_{kj}^{-2})^{-1/2}$, the
classical minimum-variance combination of independent estimates; the default
$p=1.3$ grants deliberately less diversification credit. A coverage mask
records which edges any feed observed; uncovered edges pass to imputation as
missing.

\subsection{Imputation engine}
Imputers are plugins run in two phases: a \emph{cascade} whose members are
swept in order until one full sweep fills no new edge (at most 10 sweeps), and
a \emph{terminal} phase run once. Fills are write-once (no imputer overwrites
an observed or previously filled edge), values are clipped to the per-edge
bounds $[l,u]$ with $\sigma$ widened by the clip distance, and every fill is
appended to the imputation log with its method, source edges, coefficients,
and before/after values. Table~\ref{tab:si-imputers} states the per-imputer
rules; $\varepsilon_r$ denotes a per-relation relative residual term.

Implemented in \texttt{pysuto/constraints/}.

\subsection{Constraint families}

carries metadata (family, year, cone) and per-year targets $c(t)$ with
uncertainties $\sigma_c(t)$. Table~\ref{tab:si-constraints} lists the families
of the reference configuration.

\subsection{Prior conditioning and the no-data anchor}\label{si:anchor}
Before persisting, prior uncertainties are regularised through Eq.~(1) of the
main text with a relative and an absolute floor,
\begin{equation}
\sigma_a' = \Lp{\sigma_a,\ \mathrm{CV}_a^{\min} |a_0|,\
\sigma_a^{\mathrm{floor}}},
\qquad
\mathrm{CV}_a^{\min} = 10^{-3},\quad \sigma_a^{\mathrm{floor}} = 10^{-6},
\end{equation}
and free-row constraint uncertainties analogously with
$\mathrm{CV}_c^{\min}=10^{-2}$, $\sigma_c^{\mathrm{floor}}=10^{-3}$.
Edges that reached Stage 3 as noisy zeros ($a_0 = 0$, $\sigma \ge 10^{5}$)
would otherwise act as free slack: with $(f-0)^2/\sigma^2 \approx 0$ the
solver can route arbitrary mass through data-free subgraphs. The no-data
anchor therefore rescales their $\sigma$ to
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{a,j} \leftarrow \max\bigl(\sigma_a^{\mathrm{floor}},\
0.05 \cdot \operatorname{median}\{|a_{0,i}| \ne 0 :\ i \sim j\}\bigr),
\end{equation}
where $i \sim j$ ranges over the edge's (sector, valuation) siblings. The
prior mean stays zero, so these edges are softly pulled toward zero at a
sector-appropriate scale while a hard balance can still force flow through
them at finite cost. Sectors whose magnitude is structurally determined by a
ratio or balance relation rather than by feed data (in the copper application
the extraction front-end and the residual outputs) are excluded: for them
$a_0 = 0$ means ``value comes from the relation'', not ``no flow''.

Finally, $G$ is partitioned into per-year blocks; a build-time check verifies
that no row couples edges of different years, so each year solves
independently (in the copper application, 30 blocks of $\approx$45{,}900 rows
$\times$ 28{,}482 edges).

\section{Stage 4 --- Solver}\label{si:stage4}
Implemented in \texttt{pysuto/solver.py} and \texttt{pysuto/\_conditioning.py}.

\subsection{Conic standard form}
Eq.~(2) of the main text is passed to Clarabel \citep{goulart2024clarabel} in
the standard form $\min \tfrac12 x^\top P x + q^\top x$ subject to
$Ax + s = b$, $s \in \mathcal{K}$, with stacked variables
$x = [a;\ \varepsilon]$:
\begin{equation}
P = 2\begin{bmatrix}\Sigma_a^{-1} & 0\\ 0 & \Sigma_c^{-1}\end{bmatrix},
\quad
q = \begin{bmatrix}-2\,\Sigma_a^{-1} a_0\\ 0\end{bmatrix},
\quad
A = \begin{bmatrix}G_s & -I\\ G_h & 0\\ G_b & 0\end{bmatrix},
\quad
b = \begin{bmatrix}c_s(t)\\ c_h(t)\\ c_b(t)\end{bmatrix},
\end{equation}
with cones $\mathcal{K} = \{0\}^{m_s} \times \{0\}^{m_h} \times
\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}^{m_b}$: soft rows are exact equalities in $x$ because their
residual $\varepsilon$ is itself a variable, hard balances sit in a zero cone,
and band rows in a nonnegative cone. Representing hard rows as cones rather
than as small-$\sigma$ soft rows avoids the $\kappa \approx 10^{12}$
ill-conditioning that $\sigma_c \to 0$ would inject into $P$.

\subsection{Conditioning, feasibility probe, and prior modes}
Before each solve, $\sigma_a$ is clipped to
$[\max(10^{-8},\ 10^{-5}|a_0|),\ 10^{5}\cdot\max\sigma_a^{\mathrm{finite}}]$
(shared with Stage 5, so solver and posterior see the same prior). A
feasibility probe minimises $10^{-10}\|x\|^2$ over the hard subsystem alone
--- zero-cone balances plus $[G_b; I; -I]$ against $[c_b; u; -l]$ --- and, on
failure, reports the rows with the largest violation $|G a - c|$; this is the
only place the stored bounds $l, u$ act on the optimisation. The prior is
$a_0(t) = f_{\mathrm{agg}}(t)$ (cold, the executed default) or the previous
year's optimum $a^*(t{-}1)$ (warm chaining). Independently, the KKT
factorisation is reused across years when the block sparsity pattern is
identical, refreshing only $P$, $q$, and $b$; a legacy path solving the
monolithic $G$ remains available.

\section{Stage 5 --- Posterior uncertainty propagation}\label{si:stage5}
Implemented in \texttt{pysuto/uncprop.py}.

\subsection{Derivation}
Model the prior as $a \sim \mathcal{N}(a_0, \Sigma_a)$ and the constraint
system as a Gaussian observation $G a = c - \eta$ with $\eta \sim
\mathcal{N}(0, \Sigma_c^{\mathrm{full}})$, where
$\Sigma_c^{\mathrm{full}} = \operatorname{blkdiag}(0_{m_h}, \Sigma_c)$ assigns
hard rows zero variance. Standard Gaussian conditioning yields the posterior
covariance of Eq.~(3) of the main text,
\begin{equation}
\Sigma_{a^*} = \Sigma_a - \Sigma_a G^\top S^{-1} G \Sigma_a,
\qquad S = G \Sigma_a G^\top + \Sigma_c^{\mathrm{full}},
\end{equation}
the generalised-least-squares update of the reconciliation lineage
\citep{byron1978, vanderploeg1982}. Only the marginal variances are needed:
with the sparse Cholesky factorisation $S = L L^\top$ (CHOLMOD, symbolic
pattern reused across years; a SuperLU fallback costs about twice as much),
\begin{equation}
\sigma^2_{a^*\!,i} = \sigma^2_{a,i}\bigl(1 - \sigma^2_{a,i}\,
\| L^{-1} g_i \|_2^2\bigr), \qquad g_i = \text{column } i \text{ of } G.
\end{equation}
Intervals are $[\,\max(a^*_i - z\,\sigma_{a^*\!,i},\ l_i),\
\min(a^*_i + z\,\sigma_{a^*\!,i},\ u_i)\,]$ with
$z = \Phi^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)$, $\alpha = 0.05$.

\subsection{Invariants and limitations}
Three properties are enforced by tests: monotone shrinkage
($\sigma^2_{a^*} \le \sigma^2_a$ elementwise), positive semidefiniteness of
the update, and recovery of the hard-constraints-only reduction of
\citet{byron1978} in the limit $\Sigma_c \to 0$. Hard-balanced edges obtain
zero-width intervals --- the correct limiting behaviour, not an artefact. Two
limitations qualify the result: the update is the linear-Gaussian (Laplace)
approximation of the true posterior, increasingly approximate for wide
pedigree tails, and it is blind to the bounds, so $\sigma_{a^*}$ overestimates
the uncertainty of solutions resting on $l$ or $u$; interval clipping is
applied after the fact.

\section{Provenance schema}\label{si:provenance}
Implemented in \texttt{pysuto/input\_registry.py} and \texttt{pysuto/registry/}.

Inputs live in a versioned registry of four kinds (feeds, accounting
structure, parameter tables, masks). At initialisation the registry hashes
each input's load-bearing files (SHA-256); any change materialises a new
version directory, with unchanged files deduplicated as hardlinks, and runs
may pin older versions. Every stage writes its outputs under a run identifier
\texttt{\{stage\}\_\{timestamp\}\_\{uuid\}} together with a sidecar
(\texttt{metadata.json}) recording: the exact input versions consumed
(\texttt{parents.inputs}: id, version, content code), the upstream run
identifiers (\texttt{parents.upstream}), every configuration knob of the stage
(\texttt{stage\_config}), and summary statistics (\texttt{stage\_summary}).
The chain feed $\to$ imputation $\to$ constraints $\to$ optimisation $\to$
posterior is thereby fully reconstructable from any run directory, and
cross-stage consistency is enforced at build time: a constraint run refuses an
imputation run built against a different accounting-structure version.
