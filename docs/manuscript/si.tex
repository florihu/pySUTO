\section{Stage 1 --- Datafeed assembly}\label{si:stage1}
Implemented in \texttt{pysuto/\{unc,conc,feedpipe\}.py}.

\subsection{Pedigree scores to coefficients of variation}
Each feed carries one pedigree score $s\in\{1,2,3,4\}$ per data-quality
indicator (reliability, completeness, temporal, geographical and other
correlation), following \citet{laner2016} with the pedigree lineage of
\citet{weidema1996pedigree}. Scores map to per-indicator coefficients of
variation through the exponential model
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{rel}} = \frac{0.75\, e^{1.105\, s_{\mathrm{rel}}}}{100},
\qquad
\mathrm{CV}_{i} =
\begin{cases}
0 & s_i = 1,\\[2pt]
\dfrac{a_i\, e^{1.105\,(s_i-1)}}{100} & s_i > 1,
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
where the sensitivity coefficient $a_i$ is $0.375$ (not sensitive), $0.750$
(medium) or $1.5$ (highly sensitive) according to the indicator's assessed
influence; every copper feed uses the medium setting. The indicators combine
in quadrature to one per-feed coefficient of variation,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}} = \sqrt{\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{rel}}^2 +
\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{comp}}^2 + \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{temp}}^2 +
\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{geo}}^2 + \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{oth}}^2}.
\end{equation}
Edges touching the aggregate rest-of-world region are penalised by the factor
$\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}(s_{\mathrm{geo}}{=}4)/\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}(s_{\mathrm{geo}})$,
equivalent to re-scoring them at the worst geographical correlation
(ADR~0003). Table~\ref{tab:si-pedigree} gives the scores of the three
copper feeds and the resulting CVs.

\subsection{Concordance}
Raw records are first cast as directed source edges, then mapped onto the
root accounting structure by a sparse weight matrix $W$:
\begin{equation}
f_{\mathrm{root}} = W^{\top} f_{\mathrm{src}},
\qquad \textstyle\sum_j W_{ij} = 1 \ \text{for every source edge } i,
\end{equation}
so every fan-out conserves mass. Two guards act on the projection: the
projected total must agree with the raw total of the matched records to
within 1\% (a violation raises an error rather than a warning), and a raw
record that fans onto several root edges is counted once on its source edge
while distinct records resolving to the same source edge are summed. A
record that names only one endpoint (an ICSG usage row: consumer known,
producer unknown) becomes one source edge whose open side is distributed
over the matching root edges by normalised weights (ADR~0002). Uniform
weights are overridden where external split information exists (the copper
trade shares of \S\ref{si:corrections}). A fan with no split information is
\emph{not} distributed: asserting a uniform $1/N$ partner pattern would
fabricate structure the source does not contain, so the raw record leaves
the table, is excluded from the mass-conservation total, and its edges fall
to Stage-2 imputation (ADR~0008). A consumer that the trade source observes
with zero imports is fanned entirely onto its domestic leg (ADR~0018); a
zero constructed by the re-export netting is not such an observation and is
withheld (ADR~0027). A qualifier fan --- a source variable that names its
input product, such as smelter scrap feed --- keeps only the legs whose
supplier sector matches.

\subsection{CV-invariant propagation}
Uncertainty propagates through $W$ in coefficient-of-variation form. With
value shares $A_{ij} = |W_{ij} f_{\mathrm{src},i}|$ and fan-in weights
$\omega_{ij} = A_{ij}/\sum_k A_{kj}$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{root},j} = \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}
\Bigl(\sum_i \omega_{ij}^{\,p}\Bigr)^{1/p},
\qquad
\sigma_{\mathrm{root},j} = \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{root},j}\,
|f_{\mathrm{root},j}|.
\label{eq:si-cvprop}
\end{equation}
Because the $\omega_{ij}$ lie on the simplex, $\omega_{ij}^{\,p} \le
\omega_{ij}$ for $p \ge 1$ and hence $\sum_i \omega_{ij}^{\,p} \le 1$: fan-in
diversifies ($\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{root}} \le \mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}$),
pure fan-out leaves the CV unchanged, and no concordance operation can
increase relative uncertainty. Stage 1 outputs one root-aligned pair
$(F_k, \sigma_k)$ per feed, registered under a run id that embeds the feed
name.

\section{Stage 2 --- Aggregation and imputation}\label{si:stage2}
Implemented in \texttt{pysuto/\{collector,agg\}.py} and \texttt{pysuto/impute/}.

\subsection{Inverse-variance aggregation}
With per-feed precisions $P_{kj} = \sigma_{kj}^{-2}$ (zero where feed $k$ does
not cover edge $j$; observations below $0.01$~kt are suppressed as noise),
\begin{equation}
\Omega_{kj} = \frac{P_{kj}}{\sum_k P_{kj}}, \qquad
f_{\mathrm{agg},j} = \sum_k \Omega_{kj} F_{kj}, \qquad
\sigma_{\mathrm{agg},j} = \Lp{\{\Omega_{kj}\sigma_{kj}\}_k}.
\label{eq:si-agg}
\end{equation}
At $p=2$, $\sigma_{\mathrm{agg},j} = (\sum_k \sigma_{kj}^{-2})^{-1/2}$, the
classical minimum-variance combination of independent estimates; $p=1.3$
grants deliberately less diversification credit. A coverage mask records
which edges any feed observed (Table~\ref{tab:si-coverage}); uncovered
edges pass to imputation as missing.

\subsection{Three adjustments before imputation}
\emph{Size-damped trade-leg $\sigma$} (ADR~0017). The pedigree CV is
size-blind, so deleting any leg entirely costs the same $z = 1/\mathrm{CV}$
and the largest legs are by far the cheapest to delete per tonne. Every
bilateral leg $e$ of product $s$ therefore gets
\begin{equation}
\sigma_e = \mathrm{CV}_e\, a_{\mathrm{ref},s}^{\,1-\gamma}\, a_e^{\,\gamma},
\qquad \gamma = 0.5,\qquad \sigma_e \le 1.0\,|a_e|,
\end{equation}
with $a_{\mathrm{ref},s}$ the median covered leg of the product. Because the
median moves whenever the leg population is cleaned, the reference is pinned
to the values of the run that preceded the re-export netting (concentrate
10.26, anode 0.213, cathode 0.268, matte 0.223~kt; ADR~0027). Running the
shipped chain with the median re-derived measured 1.7\% worse.

\emph{Outlier-leg screen.} An exporter whose implied bilateral exports of a
product-year ($\sum \mathrm{Mass}\cdot c$) exceed $K = 3$ times its covered
supply (ICSG-reported production plus implied imports) has all its legs of
that product-year inflated to $\sigma = 5\,|a|$. The data stay; only their
authority goes. Exporters with no covered production are not screened.

\emph{Contradicted-node detector} (ADR~0019). A product node whose outflow
prior falls below $0.5\times$ its inflow prior (matte and anode nodes are
checked) is reported with both series; the remedy once attached to it ---
inflating the outflow $\sigma$ and loosening the matching scale total ---
was measured and rejected, because it repriced the contradiction while 0.46\%
of the model's mass moved and the collapses were untouched. Only the
detector runs.

\subsection{Imputation engine}
Imputers are plugins run in two phases: a \emph{cascade} whose members are
swept in order until one full sweep fills no new edge (at most 10 sweeps; the
copper run needs two), and a \emph{terminal} phase run once. Every imputer
fills only edges that are still missing (write-once, a convention each
imputer implements); the engine clips any fill to the edge bounds $[l,u]$
without widening $\sigma$ (the network imputer widens $\sigma$ by its own
clip distance), and every fill is appended to the imputation log with its
method, source edges, coefficients and before/after values.
Table~\ref{tab:si-imputers} states the per-imputer rules in execution order;
$\varepsilon_r$ denotes a per-relation relative residual term.

\subsection{Prior conditioning and the no-data anchor}\label{si:anchor}
Before persisting, prior uncertainties are regularised through Eq.~(1) of the
main text with a relative and an absolute floor,
\begin{equation}
\sigma_a' = \Lp{\sigma_a,\ \mathrm{CV}_a^{\min} |a_0|,\
\sigma_a^{\mathrm{floor}}},
\qquad
\mathrm{CV}_a^{\min} = 10^{-3},\quad \sigma_a^{\mathrm{floor}} = 10^{-6},
\end{equation}
and soft-row constraint uncertainties analogously with
$\mathrm{CV}_c^{\min}=10^{-2}$, $\sigma_c^{\mathrm{floor}}=10^{-3}$.
Edges that reached Stage 3 as noisy zeros ($|a_0| < 10^{-9}$, $\sigma \ge
10^{5}$) would otherwise act as free slack: with $(f-0)^2/\sigma^2 \approx 0$
the solver can route arbitrary mass through data-free subgraphs. The no-data
anchor therefore rescales their $\sigma$ to
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{a,j} \leftarrow \max\bigl(\sigma_a^{\mathrm{floor}},\
0.05 \cdot \operatorname{median}\{|a_{0,i}| \ne 0 :\ i \sim j\}\bigr),
\end{equation}
where $i \sim j$ ranges over the edge's siblings with the same source sector
and valuation (ADR~0013). The prior mean stays zero, so these edges are
softly pulled toward zero at a sector-appropriate scale while a hard balance
can still force flow through them at finite cost. Sectors whose magnitude is
structurally determined by a ratio or balance relation rather than by feed
data are excluded --- in copper the extraction chain (CO, Mining, Ore) and
the residual outputs (WR, Tailings, Slag, PSC): for them $a_0 = 0$ means
``value comes from the relation'', not ``no flow''. The same exempt list
governs the flat-prior zero pin $l = u = 0$, which reaches only the
feasibility probe of Stage 4.

Finally, $G$ is partitioned into per-year blocks; a build-time check
verifies that no row couples edges of different years, so each year solves
independently (30 blocks of 45{,}416--45{,}584 rows $\times$ 28{,}482
edges). The constraint stage refuses an imputation run built against a
different version of the accounting structure.

\section{Stage 4 --- Solver}\label{si:stage4}
Implemented in \texttt{pysuto/solver.py} and \texttt{pysuto/\_conditioning.py}.

\subsection{Conic standard form}
Eq.~(2) of the main text is passed to Clarabel \citep{goulart2024clarabel} in
the standard form $\min \tfrac12 x^\top P x + q^\top x$ subject to
$Ax + s = b$, $s \in \mathcal{K}$, with stacked variables
$x = [a;\ \varepsilon]$:
\begin{equation}
P = 2\begin{bmatrix}\Sigma_a^{-1} & 0\\ 0 & \Sigma_c^{-1}\end{bmatrix},
\quad
q = \begin{bmatrix}-2\,\Sigma_a^{-1} a_0\\ 0\end{bmatrix},
\quad
A = \begin{bmatrix}G_s & -I\\ G_h & 0\\ G_b & 0\end{bmatrix},
\quad
b = \begin{bmatrix}c_s(t)\\ c_h(t)\\ c_b(t)\end{bmatrix},
\end{equation}
with cones $\mathcal{K} = \{0\}^{m_s} \times \{0\}^{m_h} \times
\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}^{m_b}$: soft rows are exact equalities in $x$ because their
residual $\varepsilon$ is itself a variable, hard balances sit in a zero cone,
and band rows in a non-negative cone. Representing hard rows as cones rather
than as small-$\sigma$ soft rows avoids the $\kappa \approx 10^{12}$
ill-conditioning that $\sigma_c \to 0$ would inject into $P$. No row of $A$
represents a bound on $a$.

\subsection{Conditioning, feasibility probe, prior modes, factorisation}
Before each solve, $\sigma_a$ is clipped to
$[\max(10^{-8},\ 10^{-5}|a_0|),\ 10^{5}\cdot\max\sigma_a^{\mathrm{finite}}]$
(shared with Stage 5, so solver and posterior see the same prior). A
feasibility probe minimises $\tfrac12\cdot10^{-10}\|a\|^2$ over the hard
subsystem alone --- zero-cone balances plus $[G_b; I; -I]$ against
$[c_b; u; -l]$ --- and, on failure, reports the rows with the largest
violation $|G a - c|$; this is the only place the stored bounds $l, u$ act
on the optimisation. By default the probe runs on the first year only (a
full scan costs about 350~s per year); each year's own solve then reports its
status. The prior is $a_0(t) = f_{\mathrm{agg}}(t)$ (cold; what the
reference run used) or the previous year's optimum $a^*(t{-}1)$ (warm
chaining; the solver class's default, overridden by the driver's
command-line default). Independently, the KKT factorisation would be reused
across years when every year's $A$ is identical in shape, sparsity pattern
and values; because the copper years differ in row count, each year is
factorised afresh. A legacy path solving the monolithic $G$ remains
available. Any solver status outside \{Solved, AlmostSolved\} raises; there
is no silent fallback.

\section{Stage 5 --- Posterior uncertainty propagation}\label{si:stage5}
Implemented in \texttt{pysuto/uncprop.py}.

\subsection{Derivation}
Model the prior as $a \sim \mathcal{N}(a_0, \Sigma_a)$ and the constraint
system as a Gaussian observation $G a = c - \eta$ with $\eta \sim
\mathcal{N}(0, \Sigma_c^{\mathrm{full}})$, where
$\Sigma_c^{\mathrm{full}} = \operatorname{blkdiag}(0_{m_h}, \Sigma_c)$ assigns
hard rows zero variance. Standard Gaussian conditioning yields the posterior
covariance of Eq.~(3) of the main text,
\begin{equation}
\Sigma_{a^*} = \Sigma_a - \Sigma_a G^\top S^{-1} G \Sigma_a,
\qquad S = G \Sigma_a G^\top + \Sigma_c^{\mathrm{full}},
\end{equation}
the generalised-least-squares update of the reconciliation lineage
\citep{byron1978, vanderploeg1982}. Only the marginal variances are needed:
with the sparse Cholesky factorisation $S = L L^\top$ (CHOLMOD through
\texttt{scikit-sparse} when installed, otherwise a SuperLU fallback; the
reference environment has the fallback), computed per year from scratch,
\begin{equation}
\sigma^2_{a^*\!,i} = \sigma^2_{a,i}\bigl(1 - \sigma^2_{a,i}\,
\| L^{-1} g_i \|_2^2\bigr), \qquad g_i = \text{column } i \text{ of } G_t.
\end{equation}
Intervals are $[\,\max(a^*_i - z\,\sigma_{a^*\!,i},\ l_i),\
\min(a^*_i + z\,\sigma_{a^*\!,i},\ u_i)\,]$ with
$z = \Phi^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)$, $\alpha = 0.05$. The prior is conditioned with
the same floor and ceiling as in Stage 4, so the posterior is propagated
against the prior the solver actually saw.

\subsection{Invariants, limitations and status on copper}
Three properties are enforced by tests: monotone shrinkage
($\sigma^2_{a^*} \le \sigma^2_a$ elementwise), positive semidefiniteness of
the update, and recovery of the hard-constraints-only reduction of
\citet{byron1978} in the limit $\Sigma_c \to 0$. Hard-balanced edges obtain
zero-width intervals --- the correct limiting behaviour, not an artefact.
The update is the linear-Gaussian (Laplace) approximation of the true
posterior, increasingly approximate for wide pedigree tails.

The stage has not been executed on the copper chain. The implementation
takes the zero-variance mask from the rows' \emph{hard} flag, which is set
for the one-sided band rows as well as for the mass balances; on the
reference chain that is 30{,}098--30{,}142 zero-variance rows per year
against 28{,}482 edges, so $G_h\Sigma_aG_h^\top$ has rank at most 28{,}482
and $S$ is singular. The required amendment is an active-set reduction:
only bands that bind at $a^*$ (zero residual up to tolerance) count as
equalities, slack bands are dropped. A second amendment concerns the
interval clipping, which reads the stored $[l,u]$ including the flat-prior
zero pin, so a pinned edge that carries flow would receive an interval
excluding its own $a^*$. Both are recorded as issue~\#27 in the repository;
no posterior number appears in the paper.

The physical input--output table is a construct family built on demand from
the tidy table and never persisted (ADR~0007, 0037). The reference
construct is industry-by-industry under the market-share rule
\citep{millerblair2009}: with $q = S^\top \mathbf{1}$ the total supply of each
product, $D = S\,\hat{q}^{-1}$ gives each producer's share of each product;
every use row $f \to q$ becomes edges $p \to q$ carrying product $f$ at
$D_{pf}\,U_{fq}$, every final-use row likewise, and the boundary rows $E$,
$O$, $P$ are carried unchanged, residual outputs being final outputs and
never inter-process edges \citep{altimirasmartin2014piot}. The
industry-by-industry calculus on the resulting $Z$ is $x = Z\mathbf{1} + y$,
$A = Z\hat{x}^{-1}$, $L_{\mathrm{IO}} = (I - A)^{-1}$; the intensity of a
boundary extension $e$ (extraction or secondary input per unit of output),
footprints $e\,L_{\mathrm{IO}}\,\hat{y}$ summing to the extension's total,
embodied bilateral trade (extraction region by consuming region), the
economy-wide MFA indicators per region (DE, imports, exports, PTB, DMI, DMC,
RMC, with $\sum \mathrm{RMC} = \sum \mathrm{DE}$ over a closed world),
output multipliers (column sums of $L_{\mathrm{IO}}$) and a two-factor polar
structural decomposition between periods follow \citet{wieland2022piolab}
and \citet{suh2004pio}. The product-by-product construct with the Suh
by-product treatment is not implemented; with exactly one producing process
per product in every region, as in copper, $D$ is the identity and the two
constructs coincide. Stage 6 propagates point estimates only.

\subsection{Re-export netting}
For each traded product $s$, hub region $h$ and year $t$, four quantities are
read in copper content ($\mathrm{CC} = \mathrm{BACI\ mass} \times c_s$; ICSG
already reports CC): $I = \sum_A \mathrm{BACI}(A \to h)$, $X = \sum_Z
\mathrm{BACI}(h \to Z)$, the ICSG intake $U$ and production $P$ of $s$ in
$h$. With $R = \max(0, I - U)$, $E = \max(0, X - P)$ and $T = \min(X, I,
\max(R, E))$, and origin shares $a_A = \mathrm{BACI}(A \to h)/I$ and
destination shares $b_Z = \mathrm{BACI}(h \to Z)/X$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{BACI}'(A \to h) = \mathrm{BACI}(A \to h) - T a_A,\quad
\mathrm{BACI}'(h \to Z) = \mathrm{BACI}(h \to Z) - T b_Z,\quad
\mathrm{BACI}'(A \to Z) \mathrel{+}= T a_A b_Z \ \ (A \ne Z).
\end{equation}
$A = Z$ is a re-import and is dropped. Safeguards, each measured before
adoption: ROW is never a hub; an origin whose exports of the product-year
exceed $3\,(P + I)$ with $P > 0$ keeps its inbound legs gross; years with an
ICSG reporting gap are skipped; a new leg is created only at or above
1~kt~CC; the sweep repeats until nothing moves, at most three passes
(hub-to-hub chains). Adjusted legs carry $\sigma_{\mathrm{new}} =
\mathrm{CV}\sqrt{v_{\mathrm{obs}}^2 + 2\Delta^2}$, with the ratio to the
original $\sigma$ capped at 5 so that a leg netted to a rounding remainder
does not acquire an absurd $\sigma$. Tonnage that arrives at a hub without a
recorded outbound leg cannot be re-attributed from trade data alone; it
stays, labelled in the residual ledger, for the reconciliation to judge.

\subsection{Ore grade}
The in-situ grade of region $r$ in year $t$ is
\begin{equation}
g_{rt} = \frac{\mathrm{Conc}_{\mathrm{CC},rt}/\eta_{\mathrm{MF}} +
\mathrm{SXEW}_{\mathrm{CC},rt}/\eta_{\mathrm{SXEW}}}{\text{allocated ore
mass}_{rt}},
\end{equation}
ICSG mine production divided by the price-allocated ore mass of the crude-ore
series (ADR~0015). Where ICSG production is missing the grade falls back to
a producer-level factor file (43 region-years); after 2022 the last grade is
carried forward and the ore mass follows production (57 region-years,
ADR~0023). The soft row's $\sigma$ is one quarter of the region's series
envelope; the hard band is that envelope widened by 10\% (20\% for carried
years). Route-specific tailings grades are derived the same way. The grade
$\sigma$ is stated separately from the band (ADR~0028). On the reference
chain: 887 region-years in 35 regions; median 1.12\%, 10th--90th percentiles
0.61--2.47\%; the table (version 7) predates the last two yearbook
corrections, so ROW still carries an 8--8.5\% grade in 2004--06 (issue~\#30).

\subsection{Build order}
parse $\to$ \texttt{icsg\_clean} $\to$ \texttt{build\_baci\_reexport} $\to$
\texttt{baci\_feed} $\to$ \texttt{build\_trade\_shares} $\to$
\texttt{icsg\_feed} $\to$ \texttt{build\_ore\_grade} $\to$ Stage 2. The
trade shares read the netted BACI matrix and the ICSG feed's own
production, so any ICSG or BACI change invalidates them; nothing enforces
the order, and one skipped rebuild across four measurement rounds cost 6.2
percentage points of scale-total objective. Each feed run's sidecar records
the trade-share version it used.

\subsection{Comparison protocol}
Every change to a feed, a builder, an imputer or a $\sigma$ rule is one
round: one decision record, one analysis report and a full re-run of the
chain, compared with its predecessor and with the base run of the study
(\texttt{opt\_20260916\_124721\_c4d1d944}) on the common set. A $\sigma$ or
repricing change --- one that moves no mass by itself --- ships only if
(a) the world retention of ore extraction (Mass), concentrate (CC) and
cathode (CC) does not fall by more than 0.01; (b) the median
posterior-to-prior mass ratio of scale-total rows with $|c| < 18$~kt stays
within $\pm 0.05$ of the base (the rows a 0.3~kt floor reaches); (c) the
concentrate export retention of Chile, Peru and Indonesia stays within
$\pm 0.01$; and (d) the objective gain sits in the $< 10$~kt anchor bands
while the $> 100$~kt band changes by less than 1\%. Rule (c) was knowingly
overridden once (ADR~0027), with the reason recorded. Because the common set
is re-derived for every comparison, the base value differs slightly between
reports (1{,}717{,}182 to 1{,}719{,}806).

\section{Provenance schema and the reference chain}\label{si:provenance}
Implemented in \texttt{pysuto/input\_registry.py} and \texttt{pysuto/registry/}.

Inputs live in a versioned registry of four kinds (feeds, accounting
structure, parameter tables, masks). At initialisation the registry hashes
each input's load-bearing files (SHA-256); any change materialises a new
version directory, with unchanged files deduplicated as hardlinks, and runs
may pin older versions. Each version carries an 8-character random code
that identifies it in sidecars; the hashes live in the registry index.
Every stage writes its outputs under a run identifier
\texttt{\{stage\}\_\{UTC timestamp\}\_\{uuid8\}} (feed runs embed the feed
name) together with a sidecar (\texttt{metadata.json}) recording the exact
input versions consumed (\texttt{parents.inputs}: id, version, code), the
upstream run identifiers (\texttt{parents.upstream}), every configuration
knob of the stage (\texttt{stage\_config}) and summary statistics
(\texttt{stage\_summary}). The chain feed $\to$ imputation $\to$ constraints
$\to$ optimisation $\to$ tables is thereby reconstructable from any run
directory; the constraint stage refuses an imputation run built against a
different accounting-structure version.
