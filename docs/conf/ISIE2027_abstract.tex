{\Large\bfseries When the gauges of Spaceship Earth disagree: Introducing pySUTO, an open,
traceable framework for reconciling global physical supply--use tables\par}

\medskip

\section*{Objective}

Fuller's Spaceship Earth ``must be comprehended and serviced in total'', yet its gauges
disagree. Copper, an EU strategic raw material, crosses borders as concentrate, matte,
anode and cathode; its yearbooks, customs records and extraction accounts disagree. ODYM
(Open Dynamic Material Systems Model) structures such biophysical data; AISHA (An Automated
Integration System for Harmonised Accounts) and the Industrial Ecology Virtual Laboratory
reconcile it by source reliability. Our goal is to distil advances in the data model and in
the formulation of the reconciliation problem into one open-source (MIT), modular
framework, pySUTO (Python-based Supply--Use Table Optimiser). For copper, we ask how
consistent and certain the account is and what it adds to its sources.

% ==========================================================================================
\section*{Material and Methods}

pySUTO runs six stages: data feeds, aggregation and imputation, constraint building,
reconciliation, posterior uncertainty, and supply--use and input--output views. Its data
model carries every flow in gross mass and contained copper, with a pedigree-based
standard deviation, so trade and industry statistics constrain the same flows. The
reconciliation is one uncertainty-weighted least-squares problem per year, with exact
balances and bounds; every flow then gets a posterior standard deviation. Stage components
are plug-ins, copper's specifics sit in a case definition, and every number traces to its
run and inputs. For copper (47 regions, 23 sectors, 1995--2024), about 850,000 flows meet
1.7 million constraints and solve in under two minutes on a workstation.

\section*{Results}

The account closes every exact balance to 1.1 kg and moves none of nine main chain steps
more than 4\% from the gap-filled sources over 30 years. The sources disagree mostly on
totals, which trade absorbs: cathode to users and anode to refineries carry 87\% of the flow
adjustment, and the totals of matte, cathode and concentrate carry the largest residuals.
Production totals can thus be quoted near the yearbooks; bilateral flows need intervals.
Customs records chronically book far more gross mass into two trade hubs than the account
can place: over 1995--2024 it keeps 45\% of Switzerland's recorded imports, net of
re-exports, and 32\% of the Netherlands', against 99--100\% for Taiwan and France.
Customs-based footprints should check hub imports against production and use. Stated
uncertainties are on average 2.2-fold smaller than the realised disagreement, so intervals
must be widened. A single 2020--24 world scrap share in smelter and refinery feed hides
region groups from none to over a third; regional recycling indicators need regional
accounts. The chain concentrated on China: since 1995 the Herfindahl index of refining more
than doubled and that of cathode use nearly quadrupled, while mining stayed dispersed; in
2024 China refined 45\% of world cathode and its fabricators took 57\%. Supply-risk
assessment must look past mining.

\section*{Conclusion}

By design, other metals and substances, such as iron in steel or carbon, enter as new
cases, as a synthetic one does. Fuller proposed to ``account wealth'' as the forward days
we are physically prepared to sustain people at a ``liberating level of metabolic and
metaphysical regeneration''; semi-automated physical accounts are the basis for such
metabolic planning.
